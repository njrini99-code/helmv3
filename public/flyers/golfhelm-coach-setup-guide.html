Coach Quick-Start Guide

Get Started in Minutes

Follow these simple steps to set up your GolfHelm account, build your roster, and start tracking your team's performance.

1
Create Your Coach Account
Takes about 2 minutes

Visit the GolfHelm signup page

Go to helmsportslabs.com/golf/signup and select "Coach" as your role.

Enter your email and create a password

Use your school or organization email for easy verification.

Confirm your email

Check your inbox for a verification link and click it to activate your account.

2
Complete Your Setup
3 quick steps after logging in

Step 1 — Organization Info

Enter your school name, division (D1, D2, D3, NAIA, NJCAA, Club), conference, and location.

Step 2 — Team Details

Name your team (e.g., "Men's Golf" or "Women's Golf") and set the current season.

Step 3 — Coach Profile

Add your name, title (Head Coach, Assistant, etc.), email, phone, and optional photo.

3
Invite Your Players
Share one link — they handle the rest

Generate your team invite link

From your Dashboard, go to Roster and click the "Invite Player" button. A unique join code is created automatically.

Share the link with your team

Copy the invite link and send it via text, email, or group chat. One link works for your entire roster.

Players sign up and auto-join your team

Players click the link, create their account, and are automatically added to your roster. No manual entry needed.

Link format: helmsportslabs.com/golf/join/XXXXXXXX

Pro Tip

You can also find and regenerate your invite link anytime from Team Settings on your dashboard.
