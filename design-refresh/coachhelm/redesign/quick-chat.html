Lane 5 of the CoachHelm redesign

CoachHelm, one tap from every screen

The Helm mark sits in the green bar beside the bell. Tap it and a small chat drops from the key. Ask for something and the page is about what happens next: the thing it is making builds itself in front of you, you confirm, it lands where it lives in the app, and you watch the players get it.

  1. The key replaces today's sparkle button. Same slot, same 44pt target, now the Helm mark. The green bar is otherwise untouched.
  2. A glass drop-down, not a bottom sheet. The panel scales out of the key and closes back into it, on phone and desktop.
  3. You see the task being made. An event drops onto its day, a message types into its thread, a qualifier fills its rounds, a plan lays out its blocks.
  4. Nothing writes before you say so. Every task ends in a confirm button named for what it does, then an 8 second Undo. Nothing is saved and nobody is told until Undo has passed, so Undo really takes it back.
  5. Finishing is the show. The card flashes once, the check draws itself, the event drops onto its day or the plan drops into its folder, and when Undo runs out you see each player get it.
  6. It asks when your words leave a gap. A practice plan starts with three quick questions; a qualifier asks how many seats are your picks and which tees. It never guesses a field that decides something.
  7. Close it mid-task and the key carries on. The key grows into a small live pill in the bar, like the Dynamic Island, and tells you when it needs you.

The drop-down · Glass, your pick

A frosted panel that comes out of the key

The panel floats 10pt in from the edges with a soft two-layer shadow and a lit top edge, and the page behind it dims slightly. The glass is only the frame: the scrolling body is a solid sheet 6pt in from its sides, so every task card, tile and line of text sits on solid and nothing is read through glass. Inside: a big greeting, four start tiles with accent-tinted icons, an "Earlier today" receipt so you see what it last did, and the Messages composer pill with + and the mic.

OpenThe panel scales up out of the key, half size to full, on the pour spring (no bounce). It is fully opaque by 90ms. Header, body and composer rise 8pt just behind it, 30ms apart.Transform and opacity only. Uniform scale keeps the corners true. The panel's size is fixed, so nothing is measured mid-move.

1 · The task itself

Four things it makes, built in front of you

Each task is drawn as the real object at small size, not as a form. Fields CoachHelm has not filled yet are quiet bars; each one resolves with a small rise as the answer arrives, and a green caret or ring shows what is being written right now. Anything optional it could not fill says "Not set", so you see the gap before you confirm. Tap any field to change it in place.

2 · Confirm, done, undo

Finishing a task, beat by beat

The confirm row sits inside the task card. One line says exactly who hears about it. The main button says what it does: "Add to calendar", "Send to 6 players", "Set qualifier", "Save plan". Finishing gets four beats, each under half a second, so it feels finished without feeling slow. Working: the button becomes an "Adding…" capsule with a sweep. Lands: the object travels home (event onto its day, bubble into the thread, qualifier into the list, plan into its folder) and the card's edge flashes green once. Receipt: the check draws itself inside a disc that sends out one soft ring, the draft chip turns to "Added", and Undo's ring starts to drain. Told: when Undo runs out, players light up one by one as the notice reaches them, with a dot on anyone who has seen it. Notifications wait for that moment, so Undo never chases a push.

3 · Practice plans

It asks what you want, then builds it, then files it where you say

A plan request never jumps straight to a draft. After reading the day on the calendar, CoachHelm asks three things in one small card: how long, what to work on, and who it is for. Anything you already said is ticked for you, so "90 minutes of wedges" arrives with both picked. Your answers become your reply in the chat, and the plan builds from them. Before you save, a "Save to" row picks the folder in Documents; the last option makes a new folder in place, and the plan saves straight into it.

PartRule
QuestionsOne card, three groups of chips: How long (60 min, 90 min, 2 hours), Work on (Putting, Wedges, Driving, On course; pick several), Who (Everyone, Travel squad, Pick players). "Build the plan" stays dim until each group has an answer. Same card the practice plan wizard opens with, so the two match.
AnswersThe card folds into your own bubble ("90 min · putting, wedges, on course · everyone"), so the chat reads as a conversation and the plan can say what it was built from.
Save toA row above the confirm: "Save to [folder ⌄]", defaulting to your last folder. It opens a list in place (no popover over the card): your folders with plan counts, a check on the chosen one, and New folder at the bottom.
New folderNew folder turns into a name field with Create. The folder appears at the top of the list, already chosen, and the confirm line updates: "saved to Documents in Fall season". Enter creates, Escape closes.
LandsThe plan page drops into the folder icon on a short arc, the folder squashes and springs back, and its count ticks up. Receipt: "Plan saved · Documents · Fall season".
Shared
The question card and the folder picker are pieces ⑥ and ⑦ in coachhelm/AGENT_KIT.md, so the Ask tab and the practice plan wizard use the same ones.
Needs
Documents is round two and has no folders yet: a folders table, create_document_folder, and a folderId on save_practice_plan. Making a folder happens on Create, before the plan's own confirm, and is undone with the plan if nothing else is in it.

4 · Asking

Type it once, watch it work

The panel opens knowing where you are ("Looking at Calendar") and offers four starts for that screen. A request becomes your bubble, then a short list of steps that only shows steps CoachHelm has actually started, never a guessed plan or a percentage. The active step shimmers; a finished one takes a check and turns to past tense with what it found ("Checked Thursday · lift ends at 2:45"). While it runs, the send button becomes Stop. When the work is done the list folds into one line you can reopen.

PartRule
Step labelPresent tense, names the real thing: "Checking Thursday on the team calendar", "Finding the travel squad". Never a tool name, never "Thinking".
GlyphsActive: 8pt green dot breathing every 1.2s. Done: a check that draws in 240ms. Failed: amber, never red (red means under par).
Done labelThe same line, now past tense, plus the one fact it found, in secondary ink.
StopThe send button is a Stop square the whole time steps run. Stop ends the run, marks the step it was on with a dash, and says "Stopped. Nothing was saved." Steps only read until Confirm, so stopping never leaves half a write.
FoldWhen the task card is ready, the list folds to "3 steps · 2s". Tap to reopen.
ContextThe "Looking at" line comes from the page (today's lookingAt()), so "him" on Jonah's profile means Jonah.

7 · How it fits today's app

What is already built, what is new

Ask already proposes a write, waits for Confirm and leaves a receipt (AskActionCard, AskReceiptCard), and the server already streams step labels (PROGRESS_LABEL). This design keeps all of that and redraws it. Three of the four tasks need a new write on the server, each behind its own confirm.

TaskTodayNeeded
Calendar eventBuilt create_recurring_practice makes a repeating practiceA one-off create_event on the existing createGolfEvent, always sending all-day and the team's offset.
MessageBuilt create_team_announcement drafts a team updateA draft into a real Messages thread (squad or player). The coach's Send press sends it; CoachHelm never sends on its own.
QualifierNew no Ask write yetcreate_qualifier on createGolfQualifier (name, dates, rounds, course and tees per round, squad size, your picks, field).
Practice planNew builder not built yet (round two)save_practice_plan with a folderId, shared with the practice plan wizard lane, saved as a draft. Plus create_document_folder for New folder.
UndoNew receipts have no undoThe write itself waits 8 seconds on the server. Undo cancels it before anything is saved or sent, so no delete is needed. Open during the 8 seconds saves straight away.

Shared with the Ask tab and practice plan lanes: design-refresh/coachhelm/AGENT_KIT.md holds the steps, task visual, confirm, receipt and undo rules all three use. Nothing here changes what shot tracking collects. Prep only: no deploys, migrations or flag flips.

8 · Getting it right

What each task fills in, and when it asks

Every value on a task card comes from one of four places, and the card says which: your words, something it read (the calendar, the roster, your courses), the screen you are on, or a fixed rule. If a field that decides something is not in your words and a read can't settle it to one answer, it asks before it drafts. It never asks what a read already answers.

TaskFrom your wordsWorks outAsks when missingWho is told
Calendar eventDay, start and end, where, whoThat the slot is free, all 11 on the roster, the team's time zoneA time with no end or length, a name that fits two players, a meeting with nobody invitedNobody unless you ask for replies; then invites after Undo
MessageWhat to say, which threadThe thread, only if exactly one of your threads has that nameA group name that matches two threads or none, a new group with no nameThe thread, when you press Send, after Undo
QualifierName, rounds, course, squad sizeThe days, the course from your saved courses, the fieldRounds, your picks in the squad, which tees, a course that matches twoEvery player in the field: push and email, after Undo
Practice planThe dayThe free window, the blocks from your focus areasLength, what to work on, whoNobody until you publish

Some things are not fields, so it never asks for them and says so if you do: qualifier exemptions and tie rules (the server ranks, and a tie at the cut is always your call), "best 3 of 4" counting, sending an announcement later, and anonymous polls. All twenty task types, field by field with where each value lands, are in coachhelm/TASK_FIELDS.md, shared with the Ask tab so the two never drift.

9 · Settled for launch

Four calls, made, and easy to flip

  • Drop-down on phone too

    The panel drops from the key on phone and desktop. The type box sits mid-screen until the keyboard comes up, then rides it. Flip: phone goes back to today's bottom sheet; nothing else changes.

    Set: drop-down on both
  • Undo window: 8 seconds

    Nothing is saved or sent until it runs out. Long enough to catch a mistake, short enough that invites don't feel late. Flip: one number on the server.

    Set: 8 seconds
  • Coaches only

    Players don't get the key at launch. A read-only player version (their own stats and schedule) can come later behind its own switch.

    Set: coaches only
  • Live pill on

    Close the panel mid-task and the key grows into a small pill in the bar until the task needs you. Flip: the key just shows a dot.

    Set: keep it

10 · What it learned from

Ten references, and what each one changed

  • Apple: SwiftUI springs and WWDC23 “Animate with springs”Bounce 0 as the default, about 0.15 reads as a light spring, never above 0.3 on UI. That is why every spring here is 0 to 0.12. Source
  • Apple: Live Activities and the Dynamic IslandCompact while running, a brief expanded moment on an important update, text changing with a soft content transition. That is the live pill. Source
  • Apple: App Intents snippets (WWDC25)A confirm snippet is the editable object plus one verb button; the result keeps the facts and a Done. Kept short enough to read without scrolling. Source
  • Claude in ChromeA plan you can approve or change, a named prompt before each action, and some steps that always ask. Here: every write shows Confirm, whatever the setting. Source
  • OpenAI Operator and ChatGPT agentNarrates what it is doing right now, asks before anything that buys or sends, and stops when you say stop. Here: present-tense steps and the Stop square. Source
  • Linear agent guidelinesAn action posts as “Searching” and updates in place to “Searched” with its result; acknowledge at once; step back the moment you are asked. Here: past-tense done labels. Source
  • Raycast AI toolsThe confirm runs before the tool, lists its side effects as name and value, and has a destructive style. Here: the “On confirm” line. Source
  • Microsoft Copilot agent rulesThe prompt must name the action (“Do you want to proceed?” fails), edits before confirm are honoured exactly, and completion shows facts beyond a title. Source
  • Gmail Undo Send and Nielsen Norman GroupPrefer Undo for routine actions and confirm only what is serious; label buttons with the outcome. Gmail holds a send for 5 to 30 seconds. Here: 8 seconds with notifications held. Source
  • Emil Kowalski: origin-aware popoversGrow from the trigger, not from the centre and never from zero; close faster than you open. Here: 50% to 100% from the key, 220ms close. Source
