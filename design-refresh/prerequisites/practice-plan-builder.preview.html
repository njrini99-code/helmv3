2 · Practice is its own tabCoachPlayerN-00 owner call 1

Practice gets a tab, and a home

Practice moves out of Calendar into its own tab for both roles. The coach’s home answers three questions in order: what is planned this week, what is next, and where are my drills, groups and templates. The player’s home answers one: where do I go now.

3 · Start a planCoachPhone and desktop

Start from blank, a template or last week

New plan asks which practice it is for and where to start. Most weeks repeat, so copying a past practice is one tap.

  • Plans hang off practicesA plan always belongs to a practice event on the calendar. Its date, time and place come from the event, so there is one source of truth.
  • BlankOpens on Blocks with one Team block for warm-up and the rest of the day open.
  • TemplateOpens the gallery (section 7). Placeholder groups A, B, C stay as placeholders until step 2.
  • Copy a past practiceTimes shift to the new start. If the new practice is shorter, the ribbon says how much is over before you publish.

6 · Builder, step 3CoachDrill bankDesktop and phone

Drills: fill the stations, grow the bank

Every station and Team block has a drill slot. The bank is the team’s own drills plus the 63 GolfHelm library drills. A drill made inside a plan is saved to the bank; plans that use it keep the version they were published with.

7 · TemplatesCoachSession, block, week

Templates, from day one

A template is a saved plan without a date (Session), a saved block (Block), or a saved week of practices (Week). Six starters ship, built from the research: college qualifying weeks, First Tee rotations, the Keiser skills test and a class-day window.

8 · Step 4, Review = the planCoachPlayerThe generated plan

The plan, set like a spec sheet

After the four steps, the plan is set like a spec sheet: title, focus, groups, then the schedule with its time column, the rotation and the tee times inside the blocks that use them, then drills, Bring and notes. Type, space and hairlines do the work. No tags, no colour bars, no icons.

9 · PublishCoachOwner override of prefill-only, for this act

Publish once: notified, posted, filed

Publishing is the one place in practice that sends anything, and only on the coach’s press. One server action freezes the version, files the plan, notifies the plan’s players and posts the card.

  • 1 · The version is frozenv1, v2 and so on. Drill edits in the bank don’t change a published plan until it is republished.
  • 2 · Filed in DocumentsTeam Hub › Documents › Practice plans, as a plan-type document visible to players (owner call 2). Republish updates the same entry.
  • 3 · Players notifiedPush (category Events) and a bell row: “Practice plan for Tuesday”, “2:30 PM warm-up · Group B starts on the short game area”. Quiet mode respected.
  • 4 · Plan card in MessagesPosted to the team chat if the toggle is on. Republish marks the same card “Updated 1:02 PM” instead of posting again.
10 · MessagesPlayerCoachgolf_messages.kind = practice

The plan card, and the words on the lock screen

The plan lands where the team already talks. The card is a doorway to the plan page; the push is two short lines a player can act on without opening the app.

11 · CalendarCoachPlayerPhone and desktop

Calendar knows about the plan

Calendar stays the source of truth for when and where. The plan rides on the event: a Plan row in the inspector and phone sheet, a glyph on week views, and a question before any time change crosses over.

12 · Print studioCoachPaper is always light

Paper a top program would hand out

Opened from Review, the plan page and Documents. Every sheet is set in the plan page’s type and order, and every page is drawn below at letter size.

  • Print CSS, not a PDF serverThe plan page with @page rules for Letter and A4. The browser’s dialog saves the PDF, so the file is the printout.
  • Black ink firstEvery sheet reads the same on a copier: words and letters carry the meaning, never colour. Nothing on paper is smaller than 8.5 point.
  • Pages that end cleanThe schedule always finishes on page 1; page 2 holds drills, Bring and notes, and leftover space goes to writing lines.
13 · ShareCoachTeam only, owner call 4

Share without leaving the team

From the plan page’s Share button: team chat, a team-only link, a share image, or print. Nothing reaches people outside the team in v1.

14 · Documents, rebuiltCoachPlayerN-10Interactive

Documents laid out like claude.ai’s Artifacts

Every published plan files to Team Hub › Documents › Practice plans. The library mirrors claude.ai: a left rail with collapsible Pinned, Recents and Folders, type tiles, a sticky bar with a segmented pill and icon buttons, and a three-up grid of cards with a tinted ground.

  • A rail, not sectionsPinned, Recents (eight and View all) and Folders live in a 232 point library rail beside the grid, never as big headings inside it. Folders show their counts; a selected folder filters the grid.
  • Type tiles, then one barPractice plan, Upload and Folder sit as large tiles at the top. Below, a bar that sticks while you scroll: All, Yours and Shared with players on the left; search, filter, list or grid and + as icon buttons on the right, each with a tooltip.
  • Cards read like claude.ai’sA tinted ground (Clubhouse green for plans, slate blue for files, the photo for images) holds a mini page that bleeds off the bottom, with a sheet or two behind for plans with versions. Under it: the type tile, the title, Published 2h ago or Opened 10m ago, and the shared glyph.
  • Shared with playersPublished plans are shared by default (owner call 2). The people glyph marks anything players can open; players get one list with no tabs.
