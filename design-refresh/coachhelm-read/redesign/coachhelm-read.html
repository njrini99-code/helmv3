One read, from the miss to the drill

Today a CoachHelm read is a green card, two numbers and a sentence. The engine behind it knows far more: where the misses gather, how sure it is, which holes, what each miss cost. This redesign puts that on the screen as pictures first and words beside them, and ends every read on what to do.

  • Cause, then evidence, then the drillEvery read answers four questions in order: what is off, where it shows up, why CoachHelm thinks so, what to do about it.
  • About ten things come back on screenThe narrowing funnel, both sides of the comparison, strokes lost per miss, shot-record coverage and more. All of it is already stored on every read.
  • Summary card opens the readTap the Board card and the full read slides in, the same summary to detail pattern as the Stats refresh.
  • The evidence rule stays strictNothing is claimed without its counts. A likely cause says so, players never see a guessed cause, and a gap says what it is waiting for.

Try it on the phone

The examples use the shapes the code already stores (the preview fixtures for Priya's long approaches, Eli's penalties and Jonah's downhill putts). Pick a read, switch between coach and player, and press Play to see the motion.

The Board card today's green focus card, rebuilt

Still the one green feature card. It now draws the number on a track against the team and the Tour, gives the cause in one line with how firm it is, says what closing the gap is worth in strokes, and opens the full read on a tap. "Why we think this" no longer folds open underneath; the read is its own page.

What is off headline card on the read

The read's own sentence, the number, and the comparison ladder: you, the team pool (teammates pooled, never named) and the team's Tour. Below it, how firm the read is, its sample and the date it runs through. All of these figures are already on every read.

Where it shows up new card already stored

The narrowing the engine already runs (length, then par and length, then shape) drawn as a funnel: 28 approaches, 16 missed, 12 on long par 4s, then the miss shape in words (9 of 12 short, 8 of 11 right). It is labelled "Observed, not a cause", which is the rule the code already enforces.

Why CoachHelm thinks so cause card

A three-step track shows which of the three cause states the read is in: Not traced, Likely from totals, Counted. A likely cause shows both sides of its comparison as dots (12 of 16 against 4 of 12). A counted cause draws the shot path and the holes it happened on. A gap shows what it is waiting for. Players see "Not traced yet" in place of a likely cause, as they do today.

How sure new card already stored

The confidence word with what it is made of: sample against what is needed, how recent the rounds are, and how many holes have a full shot record. The list of what was checked comes straight from the stored diagnosis. Coaches also see the claim ladder and its range.

What to do the output

The read ends on the attached drill, its time and level, and what closing the gap is worth. Coaches can Assign as focus, Message the player or Ask CoachHelm with the read attached. Once assigned, the button becomes the Assigned chip and the plan line says the focus is active. Worth and Starts from stay; there is no verdict or target, because these numbers have no grading rule yet.

The same read on desktop

Desktop keeps the sidebar and the glass top bar. The coach's reads for one player sit in a list grouped by where they happen on a hole, so you no longer open players one at a time to find their reads. The story runs down the middle, and the evidence (how sure, how it moved, the rounds) sits in a rail on the right. It follows the phone's Read and Viewer switches.

Every state a read can be in

The read's state is shown in its own words, never as a missing card or a zero. Each of these states exists in the code today, with the wording it already uses.

What CoachHelm computes that never reaches the screen

Nick, you were right that a lot of the CoachHelm data is not making it onto the screen. Each read stores a full evidence record (InsightEvidence in lib/coachhelm/v2/insights/types.ts). The Clubhouse screens draw a thin slice of it, and a design call in October (D8) took the gauges out, so every number became words only. These fields are written today and drawn nowhere, or squeezed into one line.

What is wrong with the read today

  • Words only, no picture. Since design call D8 every number is stated in words. "Eli 1.1, Tour 0.3" says it, but nothing shows how far apart they are, and the team pool is missing.
  • The cause is a fragment. "Not yet traced shot by shot" sits mid-sentence in bold on the green card, with no sign of what it would take to trace it.
  • The reasoning folds away. "Why we think this" opens a text block under the card. The narrowing, both sides of the comparison and the holes are never drawn.
  • You have to hunt for a player's reads. A coach taps a player chip to see one top signal, then goes to a separate Deep dive page for the rest.
  • The read stops at the problem. The drill is a line of text near the bottom, and what the fix is worth in strokes is not on the Board.
  • Off-language details. The Deep dive titles use the serif (banned in the design language), and the page has seven sections that all look the same.

Where each piece lives in today's app

The read is one new view on the existing CoachHelm route, behind its own flag coachhelm_read_v1 (off everywhere). Coach address ?view=read&player=…&insight=…, player address ?view=read&insight=…. Every link to a read goes through one helper, readHref(), which falls back to today's Deep dive while the flag is off, so nothing changes until it is turned on. Each card only draws when its data is on the read. The full build package, with the PR plan and the data map, is in design-refresh/coachhelm-read/.

What the best products do, and what we took

Research across fitness, golf and analytics products on how they explain a finding with its evidence. Sources are product docs, launch posts and reviews.

Arccos Caddie

One "Where to Focus" area costing the most strokes, the rest ranked behind it. "Lock this in" snapshots a baseline, sets a capped target and shows Then vs Now. Its help pages warn that a putting problem may really come from approach shots.

Took: strokes worth on the card, and the focus verdict with its target after Assign.

Clippd

"What To Work On" ranks skill groups by Importance and Opportunity, with a trend arrow. Coaches like it because it is shot-driven and players understand it.

Took: grouping reads by where they happen on a hole, and ranking by strokes.

Oura Readiness

A score, then a contributor list where each contributor is a short bar with a calm phrase. Advisor answers include charts of your own data.

Took: How sure as short contributor bars under one word.

Apple Health

One fact per card with its chart and a stated comparison, plus an honest "Needs more data" group instead of a weak claim.

Took: one question per card, and the count-down state.

Mixpanel and Amplitude root cause

Conclusion first, then each segment's share of the change, with a plain High, Medium or Low label and its reason. Amplitude draws the band of normal variation.

Took: the two-sided dots and the range, said in words.

Linear, Stripe Sigma, Claude citations

Every chart drills to the records behind it. Sigma shows the query; Claude ties each claim to its source.

Took: every count opens its rounds and holes.

Whoop

Numbers stated plainly, the interpretation hedged ("could indicate"). Its lighter monthly report drew complaints that it was "a letter with pictures".

Took: keep all the depth, and hedge only the cause, never the measured number.

Strava and Garmin

Strava's AI summaries were criticised as generic. Garmin names every input behind its readiness band.

Took: no sentence without this player's own count, distance or hole.

Three calls, built with my picks

These three change a decision made before. The page and the build package use my pick for each, marked as a default you can flip.

1Bring pictures back into the read

Design call D8 (October) took the gauges out so every number is said in words. This redesign draws the ladder, funnel, dots and bars again, with every figure still written beside its picture so nothing depends on colour or position alone.

Built as default: yes. To flip, drop the charts; the words are already written beside every picture. The words-only page is the main reason the read feels thin.

2What players see of the cause

Today a player never sees a likely cause, only "Not traced yet" (Q3), and that rule stays. The new funnel is observed counts, not a cause, so it can be shown to players too.

Built as default: keep Q3, and show players the funnel. To flip, hide the Where card for players.

3Team pool next to the Tour on every read

The engine can write a team pool line (teammates pooled, never named) beside the Tour, behind its own flag. The ladder looks best with both. The Tour stays the only outside benchmark.

Built as default: yes, once that flag is turned on after the first deploy. Until then the ladder shows the player and the Tour. To flip, leave coachhelm_team_pool_v1 off.
