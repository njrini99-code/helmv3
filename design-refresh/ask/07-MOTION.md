Rules for every row:

- Animate transform, opacity, filter and SVG stroke-dashoffset only. Where the page animates height or a grid track (steps fold, brief viewport, pane width, folder list), build it with a transform or a clip on a fixed box, or with the View Transitions crossfade, and match the timing; see 04 "can't match as drawn".
- Reduce Motion means Playwright `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`, iOS Reduce Motion and the app's Animations off. Under it, the page's `anim()` turns every keyframe set into a 120ms `linear` opacity fade (SHARED-6). The app's `CH_FADE_MS` is 180; this lane uses 120 to match SHARED-6 and the page, and 08 checks 120. (fixed 4 Nov: the shared values table wins: build and check `CH_FADE_MS`, 180ms today, linear opacity; every "120ms" in this file and 08 reads `CH_FADE_MS`.)
- Haptics: none (web view, no haptic bridge). Sound: none.
- Check: clips are `clips/<M-ID>__design__{motion,reduce}.webm` and `clips/<M-ID>__app__{motion,reduce}.webm` at 390×844 (1440×900 for M-ASK-28), with a JSON beside each giving the ms where motion starts. The frame time is ms after that start; a row matches when the app frame shows the same state within one frame (SHARED-12).

## Never animate

- Count-ups and number rolls: the qualifier count, Got it "2 of 7", folder counts, the step summary time and the allowance meter take their new value at once (M-ASK-78 is a swap).
- The step counter text that the page ticks during a counted read (`s.counter`): show the final figure (04).
- Re-renders: a refetch, a theme change or a resize never replays an entrance, a flight, a chart draw or a stagger.
- Charts on any visit after the first: marks draw once per answer (M-ASK-18) and are static when the thread is reopened from history.
- Blur on scroll, parallax, and the composer glass (its blur is static).
- Box-shadow, width, height, top or margin as animated properties in the build: the slot pulse (`slotp`), the avatar ping (`ping`), the pane width and the step fold are rebuilt on transform or opacity (04).
- Any entrance stagger beyond the ones listed in the table: list rows, history rows, sources and follow-ups (together) do not stagger.
- Anything under Reduce Motion except the 120ms opacity fade and the Undo ring drain (it carries the time).
- The green bar, the sheet, and the Board | Ask segmented control other than the pill (SHARED-1).

## Interrupt cases worth a test

- Stop while steps run (M-ASK-05 to M-ASK-10, M-ASK-13): the running row freezes, its shimmer and breath stop, words stop streaming; nothing is written (S-ASK.steps.stopped).
- Send a second question while the first answer streams: the first finishes or stops first; the second bubble waits (M-ASK-02).
- Tap the folded steps line mid-fold (M-ASK-11, M-ASK-12): the body retargets from its current height, no jump.
- Next then Back fast on the brief (M-ASK-35 to M-ASK-37): the track retargets from its current offset; the dots follow.
- Cancel during the field fill (M-ASK-42): fill stops, the Cancelled line shows, the draft loses colour (M-ASK-54). Cancel after the confirm press is disabled.
- Undo during the flight (M-ASK-45): Undo is not shown until the receipt; a press right after it lands reverses the slot (M-ASK-52).
