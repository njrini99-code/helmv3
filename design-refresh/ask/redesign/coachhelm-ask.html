Task moments

What it looks like when CoachHelm does something. Each one plays the shared agent kit end to end: the draft fills in, you confirm, the real object moves into place, and a receipt holds an 8 second Undo before anyone is told. Tap the buttons yourself, or let it play.

Save to [folder ⌄] opens the list right inside the card. New folder turns into a name field with Create, and the new folder lands at the top, already chosen. The plan drops into the folder, which squashes and settles as the count ticks up. Saving never shares; players see a plan when you publish.

Everything Ask can make

One shape for every tab: CoachHelm reads first, shows what it worked out, asks only what it couldn't, then builds a live draft that moves into place. You confirm, the real object flies into its home, Undo holds for 8 seconds, and only then is anyone told. Anything for Messages lands in your composer as a draft. You send it.

Same engine, no new visual needed
  • Block time on your calendar (golf_coach_blocked_time): asks the reason and whether players see it
  • Team break or class exception (golf_team_terms, golf_class_exceptions): reads the school calendar, asks which days
  • Recruit prospect (golf_recruits): fills from what you paste, asks the class year and where you saw them
  • Availability note on a player (golf_player_availability_coach_notes): asks the dates; stays coach only
  • Lift sessions wait until Lift ships, since there is no table for them yet

Tone

Same reads, same numbers

Coaches default to Straight. Players default to an encouraging voice that never ranks them against teammates. Tone changes wording only, never the figures or the chips behind them.

Decided

Decided · NickPlayers get their own Ask, behind its own switch

Stats and visuals only: it reads their own rounds and shots and draws charts, and never writes anything. Each player has a weekly allowance. The amount is never shown. When it runs out, Ask explains why and shows Request more, which emails Helm Sports Labs after a confirm.

Decided · NickArtifacts save to Documents only when you tap Save

Save opens a folder picker with New folder. Nothing is filed on its own, except a plan you publish, which also files a copy in Practice plans.

Decided · NickAnnouncements post when you press Post, with Undo

The confirm shows the exact post and who gets it. On Post it goes to the top of Announcements, and players are told only once the 8 second Undo closes. Boards and polls still land in your Messages composer as drafts, because those are messages you send.

Decided · NickSteps stay open while CoachHelm works, then fold

The list folds to one line like "4 steps · 3s" when the answer lands. Tap to open it again.
