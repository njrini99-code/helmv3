## 0. How to read the table

- **ID** `M-CRH-nn`, then the screen IDs it applies to. 06 already cites M-CRH-01 (card press), 05 (bar shade), 17 (push), 26 (switch), 30 (overlap scroll), 31 (add menu), 33 (desktop peek), 35 (skeleton pulse) and 41 (press and hold); those numbers are fixed. Rows are grouped by kind, so the numbers are not in order down the page.
- **Curve or spring** names a key from section 1 (K1 to K8) and repeats the stiffness, damping and mass, so a row stands alone. The full `linear()` strings are in section 1, once.
- **Duration** is the transition length; for a spring it is the settle time (within 0.1% of the target), which is also the CSS duration of its `linear()` string.
- **Reduce Motion** means the OS setting (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `useChReducedMotion()`). The app's own Settings switch "Animations off" sets every `--ch-dur-*` to 0 and every `chTween`/`chSpring` to instant: under it every row below is instant, with no crossfade, and loops stop. Rows do not repeat this.
- **Haptic** names the call in `lib/haptics.ts` and its iOS generator: `select` = `UISelectionFeedbackGenerator`; `press` = impact light; `commit` = impact medium; `success` / `warning` = `UINotificationFeedbackGenerator`. Haptics are not motion: Reduce Motion and Animations off leave them as they are; they follow the app's own haptics setting, as today.
- **Check** is the clip `capture.mjs --motion <ID>` records (the `--motion` flag is added in the PR, per BUILD_CONTRACT_STANDARD): `clips/<ID>__design__full.webm`, `clips/<ID>__app__full.webm`, and the same with `__reduce` recorded with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`, all at 390 x 844, light, `k` 1, 60fps. "Frame t" means the frame t ms after the trigger; "match" means the moving element's measured value on that frame is within one frame's travel of the design clip (SHARED-12).
- Touch timing (`lib/press.ts`): on touch the press state shows 70ms after pointerdown unless the pointer has moved more than 10px (scroll slop); once shown it stays at least 90ms; with a mouse it shows at once. Rows call this "press timing".

## 3. Never animate on this lane

Each of these is a fail in review if it moves, even if it looks nice:

1. **Figures.** No count-up, roll or tween on any number: team average, leaders' figures, Avg / SG / Rds columns, credits, week well "11 of 17", requests count, the badge count, scorecard totals. A changed figure takes its new value on one frame.
2. **Entrances.** No staggered first-paint reveal of cards or rows on any screen (the app retired it: `ch-reveal` stays only for single deliberate rises, none in this lane). Cards and rows are in place on the first frame the screen shows.
3. **Re-render motion.** No layout animation when data refreshes, a row's status changes, an injured player returns to Active, a class is added or a filter changes. Only the sort reorder (M-CRH-27) and the request removal (M-CRH-36) move rows.
4. **Charts after the first render.** No draw-on of any chart line, ever. The diverging bars grow once per visit (M-CRH-29). Form bars, the Steady spark and other sparks, the profile chart, the Home line chart, the class-mix bar, the credits bar and the term line are drawn at full size on the first frame and never grow, regrow on sort, scroll, theme change, Text Size change or refresh.
5. **Scroll effects.** No blur, glass, shadow growth, parallax or colour shift on scroll except the bar shade (M-CRH-05) and the large title (M-CRH-06). The large title never moves into the bar; the bar title never animates (SHARED-1).
6. **The green bar.** Never moves, fades, resizes or changes colour: not on push, pop, sheet, strip, page change or scroll (SHARED-1).
7. **Desktop peek selection** (M-CRH-33): instant.
8. **Theme and Text Size changes:** applied on one frame, nothing transitions (no colour fades across the page, no type size tween).
9. **Year coins, chips, dots, the plain "Injured" chip:** static (there is no Captain tag, Nick 31 Oct). No pulse or breathing on any status mark, the "now" in Today included.
10. **Hover on touch screens:** none. Desktop hover changes nothing on rows (M-CRH-40).
11. **The leaders row:** no auto-scroll or carousel; it moves only under the finger (M-CRH-28).
12. **Loops:** the only loop in the lane is the skeleton pulse (M-CRH-35), and it stops under Reduce Motion and Animations off.

## 4. Interrupt and edge test cases

Each is a test in 08 (Playwright on the app at 390, touch emulation, `recordVideo`). "Retargets" means the next motion starts from the on-screen value with no jump frame.

1. **Sheet closed mid-entry.** Open the class sheet and tap the dim at 150ms: the sheet reverses from its current translate (no jump to rest first), the dim fades from its current alpha, unmount by 320ms after the tap.
2. **Sheet caught mid spring-back.** Drag the sheet 60px, release slowly, touch it again at 100ms: it stays under the finger from where it is (no jump to 0).
3. **Flick versus distance.** Drag 40px in 60ms and release: closes (flick 0.67px/ms). Drag 79px over 600ms and release: springs back. Drag 80px over 600ms: closes. Drag 30px in 30ms after a 200ms hold (the samples older than 80ms do not count): springs back.
4. **Body drag versus body scroll.** On the class sheet with its body scrolled to 200, swipe down: the body scrolls, the sheet does not move. At body `scrollTop` 0, swipe down 6px: nothing; past 6px: the sheet follows. A body scroll ending under 100ms before the touch: the touch scrolls, it does not drag.
5. **Tap after drag.** Drag the requests sheet 40px and release on the Approve button: no approve fires (300ms click guard).
6. **Push interrupted by back.** Tap a roster row, then "< Roster" at 200ms: the profile returns from where it is; Roster ends at 0 with no jump; no second profile opens.
7. **Edge swipe too early.** Push the profile, start an edge swipe at 300ms: ignored (480ms entry settle). At 500ms: follows the finger.
8. **Edge swipe reversal.** Swipe to 60% then back to 20% and release slowly: cancels; `select` fired twice (crossing 35% out and back).
9. **Edge swipe flick left.** Swipe to 50% then flick left faster than 0.5px/ms: cancels, even though past 35%.
10. **Double sort.** Tap SG then Name within 150ms: rows FLIP from their current on-screen positions to the Name order; the chip retargets; no row jumps.
11. **Strip toggled twice.** Tap the switcher, tap again at 100ms: the strip reverses from its current offset and opacity; the chevron reverses from its current angle.
