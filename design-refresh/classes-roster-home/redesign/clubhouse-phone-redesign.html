HClubhouse phone redesign

Classes, Roster and coach Home, rebuilt to the Claude design

Each screen is the Claude Design board from design/handoff/, built to the shared design language: a flat page, titles inside cards, one card style with a ring and a shadow, checked text colours, 16px edges (20px on large phones) and the shared springs. Everything on the phone is tappable. Names and numbers are sample data shaped like the demo team.

Why the phone looks flat today

  1. The designs were flattened. The 8 Oct mobile pass turned the Claude Design cards into ruled ledger rows: no fill, no radius, no shadow.
  2. Nothing separates from the page. Every section sits on the same parchment with 9% hairlines, which are close to invisible. Text contrast mostly passes; hierarchy doesn't.
  3. The fix is surfaces, not new features. Every section becomes a card on a flat page, titles move inside the cards, the green hero and class binders come back, and figures get real weight. No new screens, data or writes.

Beyond the design language

Everything else follows it as written. These are the places this page adds to it or reads it differently, so the other screens can match or push back.

AdditionWhy
Section strip, not a tab barThe design language mentions a tab bar. The app replaced the phone tab bar with the section strip on 12 Oct ("Roster ⌄" in the top bar opens the sections), so these screens keep the strip. Worth settling once for every screen.
Tinted class bindersClass cards keep the tone fill from the Classes board so each class is recognisable at a glance. Depth still comes from the same ring and shadow as every card; the tint is identity, not a second card style.
Class-day ribbonOn a day with no classes, Today shows the next class day as a 7 AM to 5 PM ribbon with practice in green, so "what's next" reads in one look.
Diverging SG barThe team's game draws gain and loss from a centre zero line, in gain green or loss amber. It grows once on load and is drawn in place with Reduce Motion.
Class-year coloursFour year colours on avatars (green, slate blue, champagne, terracotta) so the roster isn't one beige column. All pass AA with white initials.
Roster team card and leadersThe team card is the Roster's one feature card. The leader cards are tinted by year colour, like the class binders: identity, not a second card style.
Green toggleThe sort toggle uses the app's own green chip in a recessed well (your Oct 16 call). It has a soft top-to-bottom shade, which the design language's no-gradient rule should allow for this one control.
Week day tilesThis week uses day tiles with today ringed and dots for events, taken from the Home board.
Score wellsRound scores sit in a small inset well with the to-par under them. Red is used only for under par.
Amber chips for clashes and travelOverlaps and clashes use a small amber chip in the card's title row instead of a tinted card or a mint row.

Motion

Durations and curves are the design language's shared set (press, push, sheet, segmented, chart grow). Reduce Motion, and Settings › Animations off, swap every move for a 120ms crossfade or an instant change. Try the toggle at the top, then use the phone.
