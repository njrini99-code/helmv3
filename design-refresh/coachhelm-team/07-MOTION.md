One row per interaction. Values are read off the design page (`redesign/coachhelm-team.html`): its CSS custom properties and transitions, and its script (`flyBall`, `setStation`, `drawTrend`, `rerenderCourse`, `openPanel`, `closeAll`, `assign`, `showStory`, `animHuddle`, `PLAYS`). Rules for every row:

- Animate transform and opacity only (plus SVG `stroke-dashoffset` for line draws, which `css-quality` allows). Never width, height, left, right, top or margin (`npm run clubhouse:css`, 04 section 8 Q).
- "Under Reduce Motion" means Playwright `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`, iOS Settings > Accessibility > Motion > Reduce Motion, and the app's own Animations off (`[data-motion='off']`). All three behave the same.
- Haptics: none anywhere on this board. The page has none and the Clubhouse is a web view with no haptic bridge at 75b4956. Sound: none. (fixed 4 Nov: Assign, from the shared read sheet, goes through `useFocusActions`, which Read 07 M-READ-09 and Lenses 07 say fires today's success and error feedback; whatever it does today happens here too. This lane adds no haptic of its own.)
- Frame times in Check are milliseconds after the trigger (the tap, the Play press, or the first frame the ball moves), at 60fps. A row matches when the app frame shows the same state as the design frame within 1 frame (SHARED-12).

## Must never animate on this lane

- Any number: the hero total, station values, chip values, bar values, roster totals, the track record's "36 of 55", course figures, Team against Tour cells. No count-ups (SHARED-6).
- Anything on a data refresh or re-render: the pulse arriving late, the brief or record streaming in, a Try again, a fixture switch. Late parts appear in place with no fade (SHARED-6). The fly-in (M-TEAM-01 to 04) and the huddle (M-TEAM-35 to 37) never replay in a session.
- Spot bars on first load, roster finger-prints, track record bars, Working on rings and the player sheet's four bars: drawn at rest. The page draws them at rest; 02 section 10.3's "Chart marks grow once when first in view, 360ms" row is not drawn and is not built.
- The Working on ring's fill on a value change. The page has a 900ms `--ease` transition on its dash offset that only fires when a value changes in place, which here only happens on a refresh. Not built.
- Coins on the hole: they never move, fade in or follow the ball. Under Course they only dim (M-TEAM-11).
- Show more and Show fewer (M-TEAM-17): no height animation; the trend line and dots never redraw on these taps.
- Station label weight and colour, the chip's inset ring, the story tile ring, the focus outline: instant.
- No entrance stagger anywhere except the trend dots (M-TEAM-14), which are a chart reveal.
- No blur, no shadow change on scroll, no parallax. The green top bar and the desktop glass bar do nothing new (SHARED-1).
- No infinite loop except the loading sweep and the spinner, and both stop under Reduce Motion. The station pulse plays twice and stops.
- No width, height, left, right or top transitions (`css-quality`).
