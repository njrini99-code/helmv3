Your team, drawn on a golf hole

Version 1 rearranged today's cards, and that's not good enough. This version starts again. The Team tab opens on a hole your players actually play: every stroke the team gives back to Tour sits where it happens, from the tee to the cup, with each player standing at the place they lose most. Tap a spot on the hole and the whole board turns to it: who loses it, what CoachHelm has read about it, and what to practise.

  • No more opening players one at a timeThe hole, the bars and the reads show the whole team at once. A player's page is one tap away, not the only way in.
  • Every audited read has a homeReads that sat behind one pill, behind flags or deep in a player's page now sit at their spot on the hole.
  • Golf pictures first, words beside themThe hole, a huddle chart, finger-print bars and morning stories. The same visual language as the read and Ask pages.
  • Evidence stays strictEvery figure says its window and sample. Early players are drawn hollow. A spot with no shared pattern says what it's waiting for.

Three directions

Three ways to answer "how is my team doing, and where do I spend practice?" Each one is drawn from the same team data. The studio below goes deep on the first, and brings the best of the other two in as supporting pieces.

Going deep on A. It's the only one a coach reads like golf rather than like a dashboard, and every tap on it leads straight to a practice decision. B becomes the Huddle card further down, because it's the best picture of who is moving where. C becomes the This week stories at the top, because they're the best thing to open each morning.

Try it on the phone

The real green bar, the parchment sheet and the Team · Ask switch stay. Tap any spot on the hole, any player, any read or any story. The Play buttons run each motion on its own.

The hole new

The one green feature card. The team's strokes to Tour this season, split across the four places on a hole (ChLadder per roster player, averaged). Each spot is sized by what it costs. Players stand beside the spot where they lose most; under 15 rounds they're hollow. On first open a ball plays the hole and the spots land as it passes.

The spot you tapped new

Who loses it, as bars against Tour with the team average dashed. Then every open CoachHelm read about that spot, from every player, ranked by strokes, each with its sample and how sure. Today a coach sees one read for one selected player. Here the Board's own caps apply per player (3 needs work, 1 working).

Shared pattern built, hidden today

When three or more players share the same step (ChTeamPatterns), it shows under the reads with an Add to practice button that opens the practice plan lane. With fewer than three, it says so and doesn't guess.

The huddle new

Ball striking against scoring, both as strokes to Tour, with Tour and the team average marked. A line shows where each player stood after their first 10 rounds. It tells you who is moving and in which direction, which no list can.

Roster with finger-prints replaces the pills

Every player in one list, with a four-bar finger-print of where they gain and lose, their season total and open reads. Tap for their sheet; it links to their own page (the coach and player lane).

Working and track record built, hidden today

Focus areas in progress, drawn as rings toward their target (ChBriefWorking). Then how often CoachHelm's reads held up when re-checked, by level, against its targets (ChTrackRecord).

Read sheet links to lanes 2 and 4

A read opens in a sheet: the comparison ladder, its evidence, the drill and Assign as focus. Open the full read goes to the read redesign; Ask about this carries the read into Ask.

The same board on desktop

The sidebar and the glass top bar stay. Two columns that always end level: the hole and the spot you tapped on the left, with the bars and the reads side by side; This week, Working on and the huddle in a rail on the right. What it takes to build

Read-only loader work plus new screens. No migration, no new column, no flag flipped by this design.

Loader (data/coachhelm.ts)

  1. Raise the coach read from limit: 1 to the Board caps per player.
  2. Read every roster player's ChLadder in one team query, and the first-10 window if the table has it.
  3. Hand the brief, patterns, working and track record to the board.
  4. For Course: add course and score to par to the season's rounds read it already does, and average the roster's standing rows for par scoring, penalties, doubles and the first hole.

Screens (screens/coachhelm/)

  1. New TeamHole.tsx (one SVG, phone vertical, desktop horizontal), SpotCard.tsx with its Course variant, CourseSheet.tsx, Huddle.tsx, StoryViewer.tsx.
  2. CoachBoard.tsx becomes the team board; pills and the single top card retire.
  3. One evidence sheet shared with the coach and player lane (EvidenceSheet.tsx); on desktop it opens as an inspector.

Rules kept

  • One flat page colour, titles inside cards, one green feature card per view.
  • Amber for losses, green for gains. No accent left-border cards.
  • Every figure names its window and sample.
  • Shared springs; Reduce Motion turns every move into a 180 ms fade and skips the ball.

Build package (design-refresh/coachhelm-team/)

  • 01-BRIEF.md: the problem, what changes, done when.
  • 02-PLAN.md: file by file, PR by PR, with fixtures and preview routes.
  • 03-DATA-CHECKS.md: where every figure comes from and the loader caps.
  • 04-FIDELITY-SPEC.md: every size, state, tap and motion as numbers.
  • capture/capture.mjs: design and app shots side by side, and motion clips, run on the Mac.
