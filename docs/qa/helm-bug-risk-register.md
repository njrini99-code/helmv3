## Testing depth by priority

- **P0:** restricted actor plus privileged zero-delta assertion at UI/action/API/RPC/RLS/Storage/Edge layers; same-name cross-tenant data; retry/concurrency; immediate security triage on reproduction.
- **P1:** full happy/validation/auth/persistence/failure/mobile flow with exact parent/child/background assertions.
- **P2:** targeted functional and presentation checks after P0/P1 stability.
- **P3:** visual/copy/rare edges only after product-contract coverage.

## Hotspot-specific assertion bundles

### Authorization and tenant isolation

Use paired A/B personas, known valid foreign ids, direct REST/RPC/Storage calls, and service-role after-checks. Assert generic denial/no rows, no timing/existence disclosure, no audit/notification/provider effect, and unchanged target checksums.

### Recurrence, time, and state synchronization

Freeze time, seed DST/month/year boundaries, assert exact UTC and intended local wall time, series graph/root promotion, attendee counts, and reload/reconnect behavior. Capture realtime subscribe/status and refetch calls.

### AI and background work

Persist deterministic provider transcripts, action ledger transitions, parent/child effects, terminal analyzed/failed markers, notification/provider ledgers, and retry counts. Text/receipt is never the source of truth.

### Imports/statistics

Use stable external ids, duplicate files, >1,000 rows, exact raw-event and aggregate totals, and independent recomputation. Assert no double count on retry and no cross-team roster mapping.
