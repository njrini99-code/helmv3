## Rules

- Do not assign future IDs just to fill the table if the owner wants a different grouping.
- Once a namespace has shipped to Bridge, it is permanent.
- A route rename does not renumber the page.
- A page split may allocate a new page ID while the original keeps its historical namespace.
- A deleted page leaves its namespace reserved/tombstoned.
