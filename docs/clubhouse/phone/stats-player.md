# Phone design: Stats (player profile, and the player's own stats)

Status: draft (awaiting owner approval)

## Layout
1. Hero: the fairway-striped header shrinks to 88px; avatar 64px overlapping it; name, class and hometown; the four hero figures in a 2 by 2 grid.
2. Tabs (Overview, Game detail, Rounds, Development) as a sticky segmented control under the top bar; swiping sideways between tabs is allowed, with a selection haptic.
3. Window switch as a full-width segmented control under the tabs.
4. Overview: figure cards two across; the scoring chart full width; legs stacked; the comparison table becomes rows of "stat, you, team or D1".
5. Game detail: the five sections (Scoring, Off the tee, Approach, Short game, Putting) become collapsible sections, Scoring open by default; charts keep their shapes at full width (the make-rate curve, the green-miss grid and the cup-miss ring all fit 358px).
6. Rounds: one card per round (course, date, score mark, GIR, putts, strokes gained).
7. Development: focus areas and goals as cards; Add focus area (coach) is a full-height sheet.

## Coach and player
A coach gets the roster pager as left and right arrows in the top bar. A player sees "Your stats" and D1 comparisons only, with no roster pager and no focus-area editor.
