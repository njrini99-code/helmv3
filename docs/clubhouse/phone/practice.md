# Phone design: Practice (draft)

Status: draft, 13 Nov 2026. No owner phone board exists for Practice. This
records the phone order the craft pass builds to, so a later board has a
starting point; the owner's board replaces it when there is one.

## The coach's Plans tab, top to bottom

1. The phone bar: "Practice", the section strip, and New plan as the bar's plus
   (the page's one primary).
2. The large title and the week's line ("30 min of practice this week").
3. Plans · Drills · Groups · Templates, the full width, in four equal columns
   (two rows of two where a label would not fit). They sit here on all four
   tabs, so choosing a tab never moves them.
4. This week: seven tiles, three to the page's width, snapped whole, starting
   and ending on the page's edges (16, or 20 from 414 wide). The strip opens
   with today at the left edge, or at its end late in the week. Today is the
   date on the accent disc, on the selected fill.
5. Up next, or the page empty (W19 B) when nothing is coming.
6. Drafts, Published, Past plans.
7. Plan format, the last row.

## The player's tab

The title, their next practice (the plan view's card, its stops and Open plan),
This week (as above, under its heading), Coming up and Past plans.
