# Phone design: Roster (coach)

Status: draft (awaiting owner approval)

## Layout
1. Large title "Roster" with the player count; trailing action: Invite (opens the join-code sheet with the native share sheet).
2. Join requests, when any: a stacked card per request with Approve (primary) and Decline, full-width buttons.
3. Filter pills (Active · N, Inactive · N, All) in one row, then search.
4. Player list: 72px rows (avatar with ring, name, class, handicap and scoring average on the right). No card grid: cards waste width on a phone.

## Player peek
Tapping a row pushes a player sheet (full height, drag to dismiss): identity, the three figures, form line, the coach's note (autosaves on blur), then Message, View stats and Remove from roster (at the bottom, destructive, confirm first).

## Differences from desktop
The card grid and list toggle collapse into one list. The desktop side drawer becomes a full sheet.
