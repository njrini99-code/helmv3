# Phone design: Stats (team)

Status: draft (awaiting owner approval)

## Layout
1. Large title "Team stats"; the window switch (Last 10, Season, Qualifiers) as a full-width segmented control under it.
2. Figure cards in a two-column grid (scoring average, strokes gained, GIR, putts); the fifth card spans both columns.
3. Scoring trend: full width, 200px tall, the scoreboard chips scroll inside the card.
4. Strokes gained by leg: the diverging bars stack one leg per row.
5. Players: the grid becomes a ranked list (name, the chosen stat, delta). A stat picker (sheet) chooses which column is shown. Tap a row to push the player profile.
6. Putting and season bests follow as full-width cards.

## Differences from desktop
The player table never scrolls sideways: one stat at a time, chosen from a sheet. Export moves to the title's trailing menu.
