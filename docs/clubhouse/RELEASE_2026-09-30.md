## Active rounds across the deploy

With the flag off, players stay on Fairway's round screens, now on the #2104
engine. In-progress rounds and device queues use the same IndexedDB stores and
payloads, so a round started before the deploy continues after it. A reload
during a round picks up the new bundle, and the engine restores it from the
device store.

## Rollback

Superseded 2026-10-12; the current rollback is in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md).

The train shipped on 2026-10-01 as `ef6e017a2` with the flag off. That
deployment (`dpl_DowNH8VfxSXvSp5ggp2ZMkHMZpCb`) was this release's rollback
target at the time. It is not one any more: production has moved well past it,
and promoting it would undo #2170 and every later merge. Do not promote it, and
do not roll back to `6ee77e98e` either (it predates the train and reopens
R-4/R-10 and R-5).

Turning Clubhouse off today is a flag-off commit and a deploy, or removing a
team from `HELM_CLUBHOUSE_TEAMS` and a deploy, never the last one: an empty
list means every team (see [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) "Rollback"). No
migration runs, so nothing in the schema needs undoing; both UIs use the same
offline stores and engine, so a round queued under Clubhouse replays under
Fairway. An open tab keeps Clubhouse until it reloads, and auto-reload waits
while a round is unsaved (swap audit §18).
