## Scenario

Anonymous load of /golf/login in six runs (1280 and 390, normal, reduced motion, Animations off), 3.5 s of settling, a duration scan, a held pointer on the primary key (released outside it), then sign in with the seeded coach and 4.5 s of sampling.

## Before

- **Transitions use only the v2 tokens and curves (D-64)**: the form's own micro-motion runs on the v2 scale (180, 260 ms on the Sign in key, quick 180 and base 260); the scene's own durations (400, 420, 500, 600, 620, 760, 1000, 2400, 2600, 3400, 3600, 4200, 5000, 5200, 7000, 9000, 11000, 13000, 46000, 90000 ms transitions and the ambient loops, 3400, 3600, 5000, 7000, 9000, 11000, 13000, 46000, 90000 ms) are the documented scoped exception to D-64 (P015 CONTRACT, CH-15601 to CH-15603), listed here for the owner; token values read: press .11s, release .28s, reveal .52s. Curves of the scene were not compared.
- **Press: every tappable shrinks about 6px and springs back (`useChPress`)**: the only press-capable control on the sign-in screen, the Sign in key, is 360 px wide, past the 240 px limit (press.ts PRESS_MAX_WIDTH), so it does not scale by design (scale stays none; its pressed look is 260 ms background and shadow transitions). No scaling control was in view to hold.
- **First paint: visible at once, no count-ups, a refresh never animates**: the form is at opacity 1 on its first sampled frame in all 6 runs; the scene fades in 420 ms (CH-15601, designed) and the ambient loops start with it. No numbers on this screen. A refresh was not probed (no client router here).
- **Page changes fade through, hold the skeleton 300 ms, one shimmer, pushes and sheets on the 462 ms spring**: the screen has no route skeleton, sheet or pushed screen. Its page change is the sign-in landing and the hand-off: after Sign in the coach seed went to /golf/welcome / /golf/dashboard; the hand-off curtain (lib/handoff.ts) is dropped by the welcome's Continue, which this pass did not press, so the curtain was not sampled.
- **Reduced motion and Animations off remove the rise, the press and the shimmer**: 4 runs: every ambient loop is paused (1280 off: 34 paused; 1280 reduced: none left; 390 (phone) off: 33 paused; 390 (phone) reduced: none left); the scene's entry runs 1 ms, the Sign in key has no press animation; root data-motion = off. Normal runs have 1280 normal: 34 running; 390 (phone) normal: 33 running.
- **Haptics follow v2 (D-70)**: needs a real iPhone
