## The pass on every screen

Run for coach and player, at 390 and 1440, light and dark:

- **States:** loaded, loading, empty, first run, failed, partial, offline.
- **Layout:** no horizontal scroll, no clipped text, tap targets of 44 or more,
  safe areas, keyboard, one primary action.
- **Loading:** the skeleton has the page's shape, and nothing jumps when data
  lands.
- **Depth and motion:** matches the mockup, Reduce Motion is still, and a
  transition can be interrupted.
- **Console:** no errors, hydration warnings or failed requests.
- **Accessibility:** focus order, labels, contrast, Text Size at its largest.

## Features

### Shell

- Sidebar, phone section strip, top bar, search (⌘K), notifications, settings.
- Team switcher. Next event card opens the event.
- Push, pop, edge swipe and tab crossfade. Back and Forward after a deep link.

### Home

- Coach: green hero, queue chips, Up next, week, team scoring, the team's game,
  latest round, leaderboard. Each link lands in the right place.
- Player: head, From your coach, week, Up next, latest round.
- Player's week still shows team events with a full class timetable.
- Figures match Stats for the same window.

### Team Hub

- Next trip, this week, waiting on, latest post, announcements, travel,
  documents, tasks.
- Post, acknowledge, RSVP, create and complete a task, each with its receipt.
- Player sees their view only.

### Roster

- Team card with filled season bars, leaders, join requests, search, filters,
  view toggle, sort, player panel, invite, export.
- Approve and decline a request. Change a status, then Undo.
- Removed player closes their profile with the toast.
- Neutral avatars everywhere; averages match Stats.

### Stats

- Team and player, every window and filter, scrub, push into each detail,
  export, goals.
- Empty, early, partial and no-match states.
- Numbers checked by hand against the database for one player and one team.

### Calendar

- Day, week, month, list, rapid switching, team and player schedules.
- Create, edit, move, cancel and restore an event. Invites, RSVP, overlaps.
- A player sees only their own classes and meetings they're invited to.

### Messages

- Pins on every device, search, filters, inbox states, swipe actions.
- Send: rise, tail, reactions, seen by, date pill, jump button, typing dots.
- Hold to lift, reply, copy, edit, delete. Attach photo and file. Polls and See
  votes.
- Details, mute and shared files. Announcements.
- Handoff from Ask: draft, own-draft toast, poll text, back to Ask.
- Keyboard, long thread, scroll, two devices at once.

### CoachHelm Board and Team

- Board, since last here, Assign, Dismiss, Undo.
- Team Board: hole, ball flight, stations, Course, Huddle, Stories with the full
  5.2 second bar and hold to pause, Inspector.
- A read and a player's page push and pop with no full reload.

### Read and Lenses

- Funnel, ladder, confidence, count-down, hole chips, Assign, Wrong cause, More.
- Coach file against player lens on the same player: nothing coach-only shows.
- Player's week, accept and decline a focus.

### Ask

- Streaming word fade, steps, Stop, read chips, sources, follow-ups, history.
- `/` list by role and flag, `@` list, + menu.
- Each flow: event, trip, qualifier, goal, note, lineup, blocked time, break,
  term, availability, recruit, message, board, poll. Draft, Confirm, held
  receipt, Undo inside 8 seconds, then the write after it.
- Confirm a round note. Two events that differ only in location both appear.
- Draft flies home. Plan pane slides in and out. A failed write says so.
- Player Ask: allowance, own data only.

### Quick chat

- Key and ⌘J, open and close, live pill, tone chips.
- Event, message and qualifier tasks, flight into the day, Undo, Told line.
- Stop mid-run; Needs your OK; a plan saved to a folder.

### Practice plan

- Brief with slide, check and pills. Draft, Swap, refine, versions.
- Save to a folder with the drop and count tick. Publish, Undo, Seen by, take
  down, rain switch. Player page and push.
- Practice in the nav: New plan, Blank and Ask CoachHelm, the builder's four
  steps, Fill the draft, Keep, Swap, Remove, Keep all, Publish.
- Known gaps: Templates, the Drills and Groups tabs, Print studio and Share have
  no route yet, and the helper's per-step offers aren't built.

### Rounds and scoring

- Start, score, discard, resume, submit; shot tracking persists.
- Round review shows yardage after the tee backfill. Season scoring, by month,
  by course.

### Qualifiers and Tournaments

- Create, live leaderboard, selection, ties, close, lineups, track, cancel, then
  restore and cancel again from the menu.
- A completed qualifier's open round can still be submitted.
- Known gap: qualifiers from Ask don't set tees.

### Classes, Recruiting, Settings, Onboarding

- Classes: import, add, edit, overlaps, what your coach sees, team view.
- Recruiting: pipeline, stages, contact, notes, calendar.
- Settings: every section saves, theme, Animations off, sign out.
- Onboarding: coach and player first run, join by code.

## Cross-cutting

- **Privacy:** log in as a player and read the network responses on Home, Hub,
  Calendar, Stats, Roster and Ask. No coach notes, guesses, teammates' classes
  or recruits.
- **Flags off:** every lane renders as main with its flag off.
- **Failure:** offline, slow network, a failed write on each flow, an expired
  session.
- **Smoothness:** Safari Frames for page switches, Messages, Stats filters and
  sheets, three runs each; Animation Hitches on the iPhone.
- **Sentry:** no new errors during the whole run.
