### Changed

- A group thread's composer has a New poll button. The sheet takes a question (140 characters), 2 to 6 choices (a spare row appears as the last fills), one answer or several, and an optional end (1 hour, 1 day, 3 days).
- A poll message draws as a card: each choice a full-width row whose share of the voters fills behind it, the viewer's pick checked, up to three faces and a count, and "N of M voted" under it. A tap votes, a second tap takes it back; a multi-answer poll toggles each choice.
- See votes lists who picked each choice and who hasn't voted. The sender can close the poll after a confirm; a closed or ended poll reads "Poll closed · Final" and marks the most votes.
- Polls keep a plain-text copy in content ("Poll: …", the choices numbered, "Vote in GolfHelm."), so the bell, pushes, search, the inbox row and the older app read it.

### Why

- Coaches settle vans, tee times and dinner in group threads today by counting replies. A poll answers "who's in" in one place, and "9 of 12 voted" tells the coach who to chase.
