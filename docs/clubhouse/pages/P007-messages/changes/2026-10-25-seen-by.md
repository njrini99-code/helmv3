### Changed

- The rule: someone has seen a message when they opened the chat after it
  was sent (`last_read_at >= created_at`), the sender never counted.
- The receipt joins the time line under the viewer's newest confirmed
  message in a group: "Sent", "Seen by 4 of 11", "Seen by everyone" with a
  double check. The words cross-fade; nothing moves on first paint. No
  faces anywhere. Polls never carry it.
- OQ-67 default (a): the sender sees the count on their own messages; only
  coaches open the list. The list (a sheet on the phone, a popover on
  desktop) shows Not yet first with when each last opened the chat, then
  Seen. Names only until the held migration answers.
- No Nudge (owner, 2026-10-25).
- Coaches get "Seen by" in the message menu (desktop) and the long-press
  sheet (phone) on any group message; a player sees their own count in the
  sheet's preview line.
- Direct threads: "Seen" sits under the newest message only (OC-3).
- The hook keeps every member's read time, moved forward only, refetched
  after a reconnect and on return from the background; a failed read keeps
  what it held, so a count never drops to a false zero.
- The announcement detail's "Read" badge becomes "Acknowledged …".
