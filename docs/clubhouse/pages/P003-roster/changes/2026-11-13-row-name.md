### Changed

- The phone row (`RosterRowCrh`, CH-3806) carries its name as hidden text, the
  same words its aria-label had ("Dylan Brooks, So, average 74.1, handicap 3.2,
  last five rounds: …"); the drawn name, note and figure are `aria-hidden`, as
  the coin already was. Nothing drawn changes.

### Why

- Audit 2026-11-13 F-0011 (S2, axe `label-content-name-mismatch`, WCAG 2.5.3
  Label in Name): the name a screen reader and Voice Control use did not hold
  the words on the control, so a voice-control user who said what they saw could
  not reach it. The coin's initials and "3.2 hcp" on the face were never in the
  aria-label.
