### Changed

- `screens/roster/Roster.tsx`: the desk card's button drops its `aria-label`
  (player, one figure, the press's effect). It is named by its own text, the
  player first ("Cole Bennett, active, ..."), and what a press does is its
  description (F-21, WCAG 2.5.3).

### Why

- Audit 2026-11-19, F-21: 8 axe label-content-name-mismatch nodes on the desk
  Roster, now 0.
- Trade-off for the owner: the old label read one figure, not every number on
  the card (an earlier VoiceOver note, "P003 #13"). With the content as the name,
  VoiceOver reads the card's figures too. A short name and WCAG 2.5.3 cannot both
  hold while the card shows more than the name.
