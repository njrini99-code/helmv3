### Changed

- `Compare` replaces its placeholder: two sides set separately, presets, the
  history timeline, the metric groups and the aligned grid with the difference,
  and the rounds behind every value in the evidence drawer. The phone has two
  side cards, an "Edit comparison" sheet and one block a metric. Loading and
  failed are kept; the rounds, field and hole reads failing (CH-16250,
  CH-16251, CH-16252), a side with no rounds (CH-16350) and no rounds at all
  (CH-16351) are new.
- `compare/scope.ts` holds a side's grammar in the address, the rounds a side
  selects and its summary; `compare/model.ts` holds the figures, the difference
  and the evidence. Sides, group and divider live in the address (`a`, `b`, `m`,
  `split`); embedded in the desk they stay local.
- `EvidenceDrawer` loads its own sheet and prefers a round's own words for why
  it was used or left out.
- Not drawn, and said so in DESIGN.md: the question bar, tiered verdicts and
  benchmarks, and the rows the metrics engine lacks.

### Why

- A coach asks "is this really different?" more than "what is the number", so a
  difference is toned only when it is larger than its standard error, and a thin
  sample says so beside the figure.
- Two sides set separately answer qualifiers against tournaments, one player
  against another, this season against last, and one event against another with
  the same controls.
