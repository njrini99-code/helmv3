### Changed

- Flag `golf_tournaments_v1` (release; production off, preview and
  development on, as `golf_clubhouse_ui`). Every route, loader and action asks
  `isTournamentsFor(role)` (`src/clubhouse/gate.ts`): the flag, then the
  Clubhouse gate. Off, every page is not found before any read, and the nav
  entry is hidden.
- The nine routes under `src/app/golf/(dashboard)/dashboard/tournaments/`,
  each with its own `loading.tsx` (CH-16401) and `error.tsx`, render
  `ClubhouseTournamentsRoute` (`src/clubhouse/routes/tournaments.tsx`) in
  place. A coach gets the season home, the desk, history, compare, the link
  inbox, the form, the lineup and the live round; a player gets their list
  and their event; a player on a coach's page gets CH-16305.
- Loaders (`src/clubhouse/data/tournaments.ts`) read the tables through
  `tournaments-tables.ts` (the hand-typed module, until `db:types`) and shape
  them with `tournaments-shape.ts` and the engines (`scoring.ts`,
  `standings.ts`, `links.ts`). A missing table or RPC is CH-16302, not a crash
  and not an empty season. Lists page past the 1,000-row cap.
- Server actions in `src/app/golf/actions/tournaments.ts` return a typed
  `code` beside the copy (`src/lib/golf/tournament-refusals.ts`).
- Nav: Tournaments in the Team group after Qualifiers, coach and player, and
  on the phone's strip, only with the flag (`ChShellData.tournaments`).
- Held migration 3, `20261024100200_golf_tournaments_rpcs.sql`, with pgTAP
  and a registry plan; files 1 to 3 mirrored under `supabase/schemas`.
