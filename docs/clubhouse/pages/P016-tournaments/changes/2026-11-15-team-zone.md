### Changed

- `data/tournaments.ts` `readTeam` reads the team's zone with `loadTeamZone`, as every other Clubhouse loader does. Before, it read
  `golf_team_settings` and fell back to `golf_teams.timezone`, which Settings never writes, and took a value that isn't a real zone
  as given.

### Why

- DF-C-07: one clock for every Clubhouse page. A failed settings read is logged and falls back to the product's default.
