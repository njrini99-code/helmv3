### Changed

- The coach's phone month lists (`TeamRounds`) name each round by its row as
  drawn, day, player, course, tee and score, through the shared Ledger row, not
  by the desktop table's label, which read them in another order. The desktop
  table link keeps its label.

### Why

- Audit 2026-11-13 F-0011 (S2, axe `label-content-name-mismatch`, WCAG 2.5.3
  Label in Name): the name a screen reader and Voice Control use did not hold
  the words on the control, so a voice-control user who said what they saw could
  not reach it. Seen on the Rounds month list, 107 rows.
