### Changed

- `HoleMap.tsx`: 18 tiles, Out over In, tinted by the player's average to par
  at the hole (D-42; level inside one standard error or under two plays),
  with the nine's caption, a "most to gain" dot, and a detail panel (par and
  yardage, the average and how many times, each play linking to its round at
  that hole, and the facts past their floors). A List view shows the same, and
  opens by itself past Text Size 1.3.
- On a phone the map is one scrub surface (tap or drag; vertical drags scroll),
  with Previous and Next hole steppers at 44 px. With a keyboard it is a radio
  group: arrows a hole, up and down a nine, Home and End.
- All time / This season, when the season holds two rounds with holes and
  differs from all time; the window is read again on the server.
- Tokens `--ch-hm-*` for the five tints and three rings, light, dark and
  increased contrast; `hole-map-contrast.test.ts` holds the 7.5 pairs.
- The round review opens on `?hole=`.
- No hole scores here: "Needs 2 rounds with hole scores" (CH-11333).

### Departures

- The selection ring moves at once rather than gliding (M1), and the detail
  swaps without its fade (M2). Both are motion-only.
