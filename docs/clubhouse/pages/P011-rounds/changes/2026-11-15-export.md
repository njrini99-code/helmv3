### Changed

- The tools row on the player's **Rounds** and the coach's **Team rounds**
  ends with **Export** (an icon button on a phone). It exports the rounds the
  search, filter and player pick leave, one row a round: date, course, tees,
  type, holes, score, to par, out, in, putts, fairways and greens hit of
  played, whether it counts in the averages, then each hole's score and par.
  Team rounds starts each row with the player, and names the file for the team
  and the player picked.
- The holes are read at the press, for the rounds shown only, with the
  caller's own access: a round the caller may not read comes back with no
  holes. A round posted as a total has no holes to read.
- While it reads, the key says "Exporting…" (CH-11480). If the holes don't
  load, nothing is exported and the toast says so (CH-11080); an export never
  goes out with holes missing.

### Why

- Coaches and players keep rounds in their own spreadsheets, and a program
  sends its scoring to the athletic department. The rounds were on screen
  only.
