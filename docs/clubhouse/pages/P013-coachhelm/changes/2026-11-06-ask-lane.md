# P013 CoachHelm changes

## 2026-11-06 — Redesign sweep: Ask and Quick chat

- Only Quick chat's card sends a message. The Ask tab hands words to the
  Messages composer (`draft_message`) and the coach presses Send there.
- A poll opens in the group's sheet (`draft_poll`). A board takes the turn's
  charts into the composer as a picture, drawn from the card on screen.
- `create_drill` writes the team drill bank behind the practice plans flag, and
  says the bank isn't on yet while that's off (N-00 010100 is held).
- `write_artifact` writes a report, lineup or recap beside the chat. Its words go
  through the turn's number audit. The pane has Save draft and Publish, and
  Publish goes through the confirm card and the 8 second hold.
- Each flow draws its own draft, with its date tile and the real rows it lands
  between. The design's route and goal line need data the flows don't read, so
  the route is schematic and the goal shows its target only.
- A player out of Ask keeps a paused composer, and Request more has its own
  confirm, ring and Undo. The alert names the player.
- Ask's dark palette is its own tokens (`--ch-ask-*`). The app's accent stays.
- On Safari the phone box follows the keyboard, using `useSafariKeyboardFit`.
- The greeting stays on the 28 step and the out card's title on 17: the owner's
  type scale has no 26 or 18.
