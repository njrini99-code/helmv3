### Changed

- `data/coachhelm-read-extras-shape.ts`: a read of a count that adds up over holes (strokes gained, score to par, penalties) plots
  each round per 18 holes and weighs a nine at half a round, and the "over the last 8 rounds" and season figures are Stats'
  `weightedMean`, not a plain mean (SC-06). A round's row in the list still names its own count ("1 penalty" on a nine). Shares
  (greens, fairways, sand, scrambling) still pool their counts.
- `data/stats-common.ts`: the unused, unweighted `perRound` mean is gone, so nobody reuses it for a per-18 figure (SC-10).

### Why

- Cole B's nine-hole −2.90 sat on the line beside his 18-hole −3.66 average and pulled the window figure up.
