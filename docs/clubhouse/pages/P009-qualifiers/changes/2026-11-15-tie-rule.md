### Changed

- The qualifier form has a **Ties** field before Scoring rules: **Last round,
  then the one before** (a new qualifier starts here) or **You decide**, with a
  sentence saying what the chosen rule does.
- Under `last_round`, players level on to par and strokes are separated by the
  lower last round both played, then the round before. Only players still level
  share a place, and only they make a **Tie at cut**. A short card stays below
  every full card whatever its last round.
- The rule ranks the board, the list's leaders, the selection workspace and its
  confirm, and Ask's board read in the same order, so the squad confirmed is the
  squad the board shows.
- The standings caption states the rule in words: "Ties: players level on to
  par and strokes are separated by the lower last round, then the round before.
  Still level, they share a place, and you decide a tie at the cut." A player
  reads "your coach decides".
- A qualifier with no stored rule (every qualifier made before this) keeps
  **You decide**: nothing already running re-ranks.
- A rule that fails to load locks the field and says so (CH-09281); a save then
  leaves the rule alone. A rule that fails to save on create is said in the done
  toast.

### Why

- Coaches running a qualifier need a published, consistent way to settle a tie
  at the cut instead of a judgement call after the fact. Last round, then the
  round before, is the common card-off for stroke play.
