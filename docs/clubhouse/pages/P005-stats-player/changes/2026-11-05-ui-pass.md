When every round is a total, the four shot zones become one, "Shot by shot",
which says what fills them in. The strokes gained zone drops its dash card, a
zone's figure no longer shows a unit beside a dash, and a zone with only a note
gives it the picture's whole place.

The phone's head sits on the page's gutter (its coin met the screen's edge), and
its line no longer wraps to a leading dot. Your game's charts start at one x
down the card, on player Home too.

On desktop the hero's four actions stay on one row, so the hero matches its
skeleton instead of growing 41px when the data lands.

New preview states: `totalsonly`, `extreme`.

Verified in headless WebKit on preview fixtures at 390, 430 and 1440. Not
checked on an iPhone or on real team data. No screenshots stored.
