# 2026-10-25 — Calendar: P-4 drag and drop

## 2026-10-25 — Slice 1: the event block, with no side stripe

```text
Design package: P-4 build plan (handoff 4.34.4, band p4) + CHANGELOG-2026-10-25
PR/commit:      claude/clubhouse-drag-drop
Contract IDs:   none new in this slice
Actions:        none
Data impact:    none
Held items:     none
```

### Changed

- Event types read by material, then a leading glyph, then ink. Tournament is
  solid green with a champagne Trophy. Qualifier is now a green ring on paper
  with a Target (it used to be the same solid green as a tournament). Practice
  is
  a pale green tint with a Flag. Meeting and other are paper. Travel is opaque
  dashed paper, so the night wash no longer shows through it. Class stays slate,
  and busy stays the champagne hatch. No block, chip or bar has a left border,
  stripe or inset left shadow.
- Size tiers by drawn height: a sliver (under 24 px) is one 12/600 row with no
  glyph and the smaller corner. From 24 px the glyph leads the title, shown only
  when the block is at least 64 px wide (a container query).
- The time line is no longer faded to 0.82; each type names its own time ink.
- Hover is a shadow layer that fades in, not a box-shadow transition (D12).
- Selected tournament takes a champagne ring (green measured 1.25:1 on its
  fill);
  a selected qualifier keeps its own ring under the selection ring.
- The block being dragged is a dashed footprint, not faded to 0.4. A clash keeps
  the block's material and adds a ring (champagne on a tournament, amber
  elsewhere), a hatch and the warning disc.
- The now line sits above every block and drag slot, with an ivory halo so it
  reads across a tournament fill.
- Month: a qualifier is an outlined chip with its Target, and a tournament the
  solid chip with its Trophy. The minor markers sit on the page in ink-500 and
  green-500; travel is a ring, not the 2.10:1 ink-300 dot.
- A cancelled event draws as paper, its title struck through, and its time line
  reads "Cancelled".

### Why

- Owner, 2026-10-24: cards with a thicker left border are banned in the app.
- A tournament and a qualifier could not be told apart.

### Verification

- calendar-event-block.test: each type's class and glyph, the tiers, cancelled,
  and the ban guard (no border-left over a hairline, no inset x-only shadow in
  calendar.css).
- The existing calendar suites and the 11 guard tests are green.
- Wants a WebKit look in light and dark at 1440 and 390 px.

## 2026-10-25 — Slices 2 to 4: the engine, held moves, resize, series

```text
Design package: P-4 build plan + CHANGELOG-2026-10-25 (P-04)
PR/commit:      claude/clubhouse-drag-drop
Contract IDs:   CH-6018, CH-6019, CH-6020, CH-6021, CH-6022, CH-6805 new;
                CH-6015 wording
Actions:        updateGolfEvent (expectedUpdatedAt, endTimezoneOffset,
                result codes); editRecurringEvent (expectedUpdatedAt);
                notifyEventSeriesUpdate (new)
Data impact:    the loader reads golf_events.updated_at as each event's version
Held items:     20261025030000_golf_events_touch_updated_at.sql (HOLD)
```

### Changed

- The drag maths moved to `drag-model.ts` (pure). The time is read from the
  pointer against the grid's current rect on every move, scroll and auto-scroll
  step, never from a `dy` at the start.
- `use-time-drag.ts` is the desktop engine. Press and move 5 px: the event
  becomes a dashed footprint, and the card (full material, scaled to 1.02, on
  the overlay shadow) snaps to the landing quarter and glides there on the
  smooth spring, retargeted mid-glide. The time pill rides above it and flips
  below near the visible top; a clash adds a second line. The column under the
  card takes a faint wash and its numeral goes green. Escape cancels. Alt gives
  5-minute steps.
- Resize grips (a full-width strip at each edge, with a 20 by 4 pill shown on
  hover and on the selected block) change one edge. The minimum is 15 minutes,
  and the end never passes 24:00. A block under 32 px has only the bottom
  grip. Grips never open the event.
- Keyboard: Alt+Up and Alt+Down move 15 minutes, Alt+Shift+Up and
  Alt+Shift+Down change the end, and Alt+Left and Alt+Right move a day. Focus
  stays on the moved block by id. Command or Control Z undoes the held move.
- Auto-scroll runs in a 56 px zone under the sticky week header, at
  720·(1 − d/zone)² px/s with each frame's time step capped at 50 ms. It goes
  through `chDragScrollBy`, which adds to Lenis's target while Lenis glides and
  never resets it (`canvasScrollNow` is not used). At the grid's last hour the
  grid grows an hour every 400 ms, up to 24:00 (and down to 0:00 at the top).
- Held moves: further moves of the same event inside the Undo window coalesce
  into one write. Every write sends the version read (`updated_at`, an opaque
  string, sent back untouched). Results:
  - stale: CH-6018, with Move again.
  - gone: CH-6022.
  - refused by role: CH-6021.
  - a failed resize: CH-6020.
  - a failed series move: CH-6019.
  - offline when the window closes: the move waits for the connection.
  The notice says who will be told: an event that asks for replies tells its
  invitees, in the Bell (`who-hears.ts`). It names a clash with its count of
  players. Each completed move is said once in a polite live region (CH-6805).
- Series events drag. A drop asks "This event" (the default) or "This and
  following". After a day change only "This event" can be chosen, because the
  series rule would keep the old weekday. "This and following" writes through
  `editRecurringEvent` and tells each player once (`notifyEventSeriesUpdate`),
  not once per occurrence. Its row is keyed
  `event_updated:series:{root}:{start}`
  so it passes the `notification_type` CHECK (which admits `event_updated:%`),
  and a refused insert throws so the caller logs it.
- Server: `updateGolfEvent` takes `expectedUpdatedAt` and
  `endTimezoneOffset`, returns `code: 'stale' | 'gone' | 'forbidden'` (with the
  current row on stale) and `data.updatedAt` on success. `editRecurringEvent`
  refuses a target row that changed. Both work before and after the held
  trigger.

### Owner calls taken at their defaults

- OC-1 hold 450 ms shared with the peek.
- OC-2 no haptic on the drop.
- OC-4 the mouse card snaps and glides.
- OC-5 auto-scroll stays on under Reduce Motion.
- OC-11 a lineup-locked round does not lift (P-5).

### Verification

- drag-model.test: snap, edges, columns, days, spans, clash copy, auto-scroll
  in px/s, labels, what drags, series scopes, payload offsets, who hears.
- calendar-resize.test: a mouse drag across days with footprint, card, pill
  and target column; Escape; both grips; Alt fine steps; a grip never opens.
- calendar-held-moves.test: one write per burst with the version, another event
  flushes, Command Z, CH-6805, Alt+Shift, and the series question (both scopes,
  another day, Escape).
- calendar.test: CH-6018 to CH-6022.
- calendar-move-concurrency.test (server): token in and out, stale with current,
  gone, forbidden, the end's own offset, no version as before, the series stale
  check, one Bell row per player for twelve occurrences.
- pgTAP `golf_events_touch_updated_at.sql` (9) is written and not run; it runs
  in the end batch with `npm run test:rls`.

## 2026-10-25 — Slice 5: bars in the week's all-day lane

```text
PR/commit:      claude/clubhouse-drag-drop
Contract IDs:   60104 (wording: a run drags as a bar)
Data impact:    none
```

### Changed

- All-day events and timed runs over several days draw as one bar across the
  days they cover, stacked in 26 px rows, longest first (OC-3 default). A run
  shows its start time at the left end and its end time at the right; its
  pieces leave the time grid, but its hours still count for clashes. An end
  cut off by the week shows a chevron and has no grip.
- A bar drags by whole days with its times kept, and a grip at either end moves
  that end (at least one day). Alt+Left and Alt+Right move a bar by a day;
  Alt+Shift+Right and Alt+Shift+Left change its last day. The notice reads
  "Moved to Fri 17 – Sun 19", "Now ends Sun 19" or "Now starts Thu 16".

### Verification

- calendar-bars.test: the run is one bar with its times; rows and the chevron;
  a drag by days; the end grip; the keyboard; respan matches the loader.

## 2026-10-25 — Slice 6: Month drag

```text
PR/commit:      claude/clubhouse-drag-drop
Contract IDs:   CH-6806 new
Data impact:    none
```

### Changed

- The desktop Month is a `role="grid"` of week rows and gridcells. Each day
  holds its numeral button, which opens the day, and one button per chip, so
  no button sits inside another. The grid is a single tab stop: the arrow keys
  move between days, and the focused day's chips take Tab.
- A multi-day event is drawn once per week row as a ribbon across its days,
  with a chevron cut where the week breaks.
- A coach drags a chip to another day and its time is kept; a ribbon keeps its
  length and times. dnd-kit runs it: pointer after 5 px, touch after a 450 ms
  hold, and the keyboard (Space picks up, the arrow keys move a day or a week
  inside the grid, Space drops, Escape cancels). Alt+Left and Alt+Right also
  move a chip a day. dnd-kit's own auto-scroll is off. The overlay lifts the
  chip to 1.02 with no tilt, the pill reads "Fri 17 · 8:42 AM", and the target
  cell takes a green ring. The drop settles on the settle spring, and there is
  no drop animation under Reduce Motion. Moves go through the same held moves
  and series question as the week.
- CH-6806: picking up, moving over a day, the drop and a cancel are each
  announced.

### Verification

- calendar-month-drag.test: the grid roles and that no button nests in a
  button, ribbons broken at the week, roving focus, Alt+Right keeping the time,
  no drag for a player, and the CH-6806 strings.

## 2026-10-25 — Slice 7: Phone Day drag

```text
PR/commit:      claude/clubhouse-drag-drop
Contract IDs:   none new (CH-6805 says the move, as on desktop)
Data impact:    none
```

### Changed

- A coach holds a movable event row in the phone Day for 450 ms. It lifts in
  place with the peek's single `select` tick and no `press`. If the finger
  comes up without moving, the row settles and the peek opens, and the peek's
  keys answer the very next tap. If the finger moves 10 px, the day unfolds
  into its hours (64 px an hour) under the finger. The card then follows the
  finger 1:1, with up to 12 px of sideways give, and a landing slot snaps to
  the quarter. Each new quarter ticks `hapticScrub('step')`, at most once
  every 80 ms. On release the move is held for Undo through the same held
  moves as desktop. The timeline folds back into the list 600 ms later, and
  the page's day follows the card's after the fold.
- None of these lift: a hold within 24 px of the screen's edge, a player's
  row, a class or busy row, a cancelled event, and a row while offline. All of
  them open the peek as before.
- Another day: hold the card over a day in the strip for 500 ms, or at the
  screen's edge for 600 ms (repeating). While the card is held, the strip is
  sticky under the top bar.
- `DayView` owns the gesture. The engine (`day-timeline.tsx`) and the timeline
  layer sit above the pager and the day's swap, and the list stays in the
  document under the timeline, so the touched row is never unmounted. It
  mounts one non-passive `touchmove` on the view's root, which holds iOS still
  only while a card is in hand and reads the finger. The document scrolls on
  the phone, so the unfold, auto-scroll (64 px zones) and growth move the
  window.
- Both pagers freeze while a card is held (`ScrollPager` `frozen`, passed
  through `WeekStrip`).
- Pull to refresh yields to the hold through the new `lib/touch-claim.ts`. It
  waits while a row's hold may still lift, and stays off once the row has
  lifted or the move was taken.
- `PeekTarget` gains `lift`. `SLOP_PX` goes from 8 to 10. A capture-phase
  scroll cancels the hold until the lift, and the cancel is removed at the
  lift.
- Adjust time: the peek has "Adjust time" (coach, movable, timed event). It
  opens the timeline with 28 by 5 grips with 44 by 44 hit areas, a body that
  moves without a hold, and a bar of Cancel and Done. Done holds the move or
  resize.
- First-run hint: "Hold an event to move it." appears under the day heading
  with a 44 px close. It goes after the first drag or the close and is
  remembered per device (`ch-cal-drag-hint`).
- New token: `--ch-ring-drop` for a drop target's ring (the month cell and
  the strip day). The tournament sheen and the class hover ring moved to
  `--ch-ev-sheen` and `--ch-ev-class-ring-hover`, and the pill uses
  `--ch-elevation-raised` (floating is retired). These keep the shadow
  ratchets.

### Not built in this slice

- The unfold and fold FLIP (M6 and M11). The swap is instant, and the card is
  still placed under the finger.
- Touch on the desktop layout (4.6, iPad and a phone in landscape). There a
  hold opens the peek as before.
- A day outside the loaded range shows the waiting heading but does not wait
  for its week before the drop, so the drop goes without clash lighting.

### Verification

- calendar-drag-touch.test (11): 449 ms does nothing, and at 450 ms the row
  lifts with one `select` and no `press`. A 10 px move or a scroll cancels the
  hold. A release opens the peek. The edge zone and player rows don't lift.
  The unfold keeps the touched row connected and freezes the pagers. Steps are
  throttled to one per 80 ms. Also covered: the drop and the fold after
  600 ms, a drop on the same slot writing nothing, the strip spring-load and
  the deferred day change, the edge turn and its repeat, an Adjust time
  resize, and the hint.
- native.test: pull to refresh neither pulls nor prevents while a claim is
  pending or lifted.
- `e2e/clubhouse-calendar-drag-touch.spec.ts` is written for the WebKit end
  batch and has not been run.
