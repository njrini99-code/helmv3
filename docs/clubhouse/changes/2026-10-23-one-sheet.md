### Changed

- One grab handle, `.ch-grab` (36 by 5, a 2.5px corner,
  `--ch-grab-bg`: ivory-300, a soft ivory at night). The phone modal, More,
  the CoachHelm asks sheet, the bell stack (an ivory handle on its green),
  Settings, Recruiting and the course view place it; `--hit` widens the
  catch area where a handle drags. Before: four widths, four fills.
- `ui/FormSheet.tsx`: one FormSheet (Cancel, a title, Save), ListSheet (a
  title and Done) and ActionSheet (the destructive choice in red, Cancel
  apart), with one set of classes (`.ch-fsheet*`). Settings keeps its
  unsaved-edit guard on top; Recruiting's three sheets are thin uses of it.
- Recruiting's sheets take Settings' finish: no rule under the bar, the
  action sheet as an alert dialog with focus on Cancel. Gaps are 16 (the
  spacing scale) in both, where Settings had 18.

### Why

- Two copies of the same three sheets had drifted (rules, gaps, roles), and
  each sheet drew its own handle.

### Verification

- New `one-sheet-grab.test.ts` (no stylesheet draws its own handle, no raw
  sheet radius, every handle is `.ch-grab`, both screens on the one sheet).
- Settings, dialog-lifetime and popup-layout tests pass (the recruiting
  action sheet is now found by its alertdialog role). WebKit at 390: all
  six lab sheets before and after; the invite sheet's grab computed 36 by
  5 at a 2.5px corner and hidden on desktop.
