### Changed

- Back swipe (`shell/edge-swipe.ts`, `shell/use-route-swipe.ts`,
  `shell/use-edge-swipe.ts`, `shell/phone-chrome.tsx`): a released swipe
  finishes from where it is over 520 × (1 − progress) ms, at least 35% of 520
  (182 ms), on the iOS curve; a cancel goes home on the same curve. The
  thresholds are `CH_SWIPE` (20 px edge, 0.5 progress, 0.7 px/ms); the local
  copies are gone. Release velocity only decides commit or cancel; the finish is
  not velocity-matched. No haptic on a back swipe. Under Reduce Motion the
  interactive swipe is off and Back stays.
- Section strip (`shell/SectionStrip.tsx`): content swaps at once, the pill
  slides 260 ms on the iOS curve (width still animates, MAC-17), the strip
  scrolls a clipped tab into view, one selection tick per change, and a tap on
  the current tab scrolls to the top (instant under Reduce Motion).
- Segmented (`ui/Segmented.tsx`, `lib/slide-plate.ts`): the thumb rides 260 ms
  on the iOS curve, content swaps at once, one selection tick on a change. The
  `plate` prop is a retiring no-op.
- Large title (`shell/large-title.tsx`, `styles/shell.css`): the bar's title is
  hidden at rest and fades in from 36 to 52 px of scroll, on the scroll timeline
  where Safari has one and from the script before that. The tucked attribute and
  its IntersectionObserver are gone; one keyframe (`ch-lt-under`) serves both
  titles.
- Reduce Motion (`lib/reduced-motion.ts`, `lib/nav-motion.ts`,
  `shell/RouteFrame.tsx`): one shared listener on the OS setting; on the phone a
  push, a pop and a section switch become the 180 ms crossfade; the segmented
  thumb and strip pill fade over 180 ms instead of travelling.
- `styles/shell.css`: 4526 lines before, 4546 after; `@keyframes` 15 and 15,
  `infinite` 4 and 4, retiring-token uses 77 and 77.

### Why

- The owner's navigation spec (3.3): the interactive pop finishes from its
  progress, the strip and segmented move on the one curve, the bar title follows
  the scroll. 3.10 and 3.11 for the haptics and the fade.
