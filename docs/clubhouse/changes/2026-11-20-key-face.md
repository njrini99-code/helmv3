### Changed

- Every `ui/Button` key carries a state layer: its own ink over its
  fill, at 7% under the pointer and 12% while pressed. Only the
  layer's opacity moves, on the quick beat (none under Reduce
  Motion).
- A layered key's fill no longer changes colour on hover or press.
  The primary and secondary hover and press fills point back at the
  resting fill, and a ghost stays transparent.
- A hand-written `.ch-btn` without the layer keeps its variant's own
  hover and press fills.

### Not done

- Page stylesheets that give a key their own hover fill still apply
  it on top of the layer. Home's "Message team" is one; it needs a
  per-screen pass.
