### Changed

- New `ui/DataTable`: a named table whose header can stay under the top
  bar, headers that sort (`aria-sort`), one Tab stop on the selected row
  (`aria-current`), and Up, Down, J, K, Home and End between rows. With
  `onSelect` the selection follows the keys. Roster is its first user.
- `lib/roving.ts`: an opt-in `letters` walks rows with J and K, and a row
  marked `aria-current` keeps the Tab stop.
- S6: the hovers on stat terms and Tournaments (crumb, ledger row, desk
  and feature links, the compare divider) sit under
  `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, so a tap leaves nothing lit. A
  ratchet (`hover-guards.test.ts`) holds each sheet's unguarded count.
