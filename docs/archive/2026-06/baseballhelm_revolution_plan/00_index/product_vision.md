# Product Vision

**Recommended positioning:** BaseballHelm is the import-first, AI-assisted operating system for college baseball programs: Teamworks-level organization, baseball-specific development depth, and CoachHelm intelligence without enterprise bloat.

## One-sentence product promise

BaseballHelm helps a college baseball staff know what is happening across the roster, what needs attention today, and what each player should work on next — without forcing the program to abandon its existing tools.

## Target customer

Small and mid-sized college baseball programs first: JUCO, NCAA DII/DIII, NAIA, high-academic programs, and budget-conscious DI programs that lack large analytics staffs or dedicated operations systems.

## Primary buyer

Head coach, associate head coach, director of operations, or baseball-specific administrator who feels the pain of fragmented spreadsheets, texts, practice plans, stat PDFs, lift reports, class schedules, and player notes.

## Primary users

- Head coach: program-wide command view.
- Assistant coaches: position/player development workflows.
- Pitching coach: bullpen, workload, pitch metrics, availability.
- Hitting coach: cage sessions, approach, game trends, swing metrics.
- Strength coach: lift assignment, compliance, readiness, soreness.
- Director of operations: calendar, travel, logistics, forms, acknowledgements.
- Players: daily schedule, tasks, lifts, practice, development feedback, goals.

## Premium differentiator

The app becomes the **timeline and command layer** that connects operations, development, performance, academics, and AI briefs around the player.
