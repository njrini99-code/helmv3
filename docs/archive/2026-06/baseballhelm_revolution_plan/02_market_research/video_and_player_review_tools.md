# Video And Player Review Tools

## Market pattern

Hudl, OnForm, CoachNow, BATS, Synergy-style tools focus on video storage, annotation, sharing, and review. BaseballHelm should not attempt full video editing in Phase 1.

## BaseballHelm approach

- Store video links and uploaded references.
- Attach video to practice blocks, hitting sessions, pitching sessions, games, and player timeline notes.
- Support tags: swing, bullpen, outing, defense, baserunning, arm care, mechanical note.
- Add coach comments and player reflections.
- Later: lightweight side-by-side comparison or annotation metadata.
