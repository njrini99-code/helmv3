# College Athletics Operating Systems

## What these tools own

Teamworks-style and ARMS-style platforms own department-wide communication, calendar, travel, roster, forms, compliance, recruiting logs, academics, and athlete profile workflows. These products are strong because athletic departments need centralized accountability and cross-sport visibility.

## Strengths

- Calendars and schedules across athletes/staff.
- Team communication and announcements.
- Travel itineraries and travel-party management.
- File/forms workflows and acknowledgement trails.
- Academic advisor workflows and travel letters.
- Compliance/recruiting accountability.

## Weaknesses for BaseballHelm's target customer

- Enterprise breadth can feel heavy for one baseball staff.
- Generic sport model does not understand bullpens, hitters, two-way players, pitch counts, arm care, cage groups, or QABs.
- Baseball player development data remains siloed in other systems.
- Smaller programs may not have budget, onboarding capacity, or admin support.

## Build recommendation

Build a lightweight baseball-specific version of:

- Calendar/event model.
- Announcements and acknowledgements.
- Travel itinerary builder.
- Forms/tasks as simple checklists.
- Class conflict and travel-conflict reports.
- Roster profile identity.

Do not build full compliance workflows in Phase 1. Support imports and manual tracking.
