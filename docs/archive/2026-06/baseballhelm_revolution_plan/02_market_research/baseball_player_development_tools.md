# Baseball Player Development Tools

## Market pattern

TrackMan, Rapsodo, Yakkertech, Blast, Diamond Kinetics, HitTrax, BaseballCloud, Driveline TRAQ, Pocket Radar, ArmCare, Hudl/OnForm/CoachNow all generate valuable but siloed data.

## BaseballHelm opportunity

Build the longitudinal player development timeline. Do not try to be the device. Organize the outputs from devices and coach observations around the player.

## Native BaseballHelm concepts

- Player development timeline.
- Hitting development profile.
- Pitching development profile.
- Arm care profile.
- Velocity history.
- Swing metrics history.
- Pitch design notes.
- Bullpen notes.
- Cage session notes.
- Practice performance notes.
- Coach observations.
- Player self-reflections.
- AI-generated development summaries.
- AI risk flags.
- AI next best focus.
- AI what changed this week.

## Import data

Pitching imports: velocity, pitch type, spin, IVB, HB, extension, release height/side, strike %, zone %, pitch result, session date.

Hitting imports: bat speed, attack angle, time to contact, rotational acceleration, exit velocity, launch angle, distance, spray, pitch type, contact result.

## Player UX

Players should see simple trends, goals, and next actions, not overwhelming metric tables. Coaches can see advanced tables and raw rows.
