# Future Product Vision

**Recommended positioning:** BaseballHelm is the import-first, AI-assisted operating system for college baseball programs: Teamworks-level organization, baseball-specific development depth, and CoachHelm intelligence without enterprise bloat.

BaseballHelm should become the daily operating surface for a college program. The app should feel like a staff meeting, practice binder, roster book, performance report, lift compliance dashboard, and AI analyst combined into one clean system.

## Product pillars

1. Program command.
2. Roster identity.
3. Practice and development.
4. Performance/lifting readiness.
5. Import-first intelligence.
6. CoachHelm AI.
