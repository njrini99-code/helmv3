# 2026 Live Research Addendum

This addendum updates the market refresh with a few high-impact live findings checked on 2026-06-23.

## Teamworks Is Broader Than A Team App

Teamworks now positions itself as the "Operating System for Sports" with department-wide reach across athlete lifecycle, communications, performance, academics, recruiting/compliance, nutrition, and acquired products. Public Teamworks materials cite thousands of teams and hundreds of college athletic departments.

BaseballHelm implication:

- Do not try to beat Teamworks as an athletic department ERP.
- Win below the department layer by being baseball-specific, faster to adopt, and useful even when a program is stuck with spreadsheets and vendor exports.
- Make Teamworks an objection answer: BaseballHelm is the baseball staff room and player development layer, not the enterprise compliance suite.

Source:

- https://teamworks.com/
- https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/teamworks-closes-65-million-series-e-and-acquires-arms-software-301794703.html

## Teamworks Acquisitions Reinforce The Build/Import/Ignore Strategy

Teamworks has assembled Hub, INFLCR, Notemeal, Smartabase, Retain, Pulse, ARMS/Compliance + Recruiting, and other athlete lifecycle tools through acquisition. This proves the market values consolidation, but it also creates platform complexity.

BaseballHelm implication:

- Consolidate at the baseball decision layer, not by copying every product category.
- Keep Compliance, Recruiting, Nutrition, and full AMS features deferred or attachment/import-only.
- Turn "platform overload" into a wedge: simple daily baseball operations for programs that do not need a department-wide rollout.

## College Baseball Stats Workflows Are In Flux

2026 reporting around college baseball scoring software indicates continued friction around StatCrew XML expiration, NCAA LiveStats timing, and official box-score workflows. GameChanger offers college XML export for staff on college baseball/softball teams, and PrestoStats markets automatic season/player/game stats and NCAA/site data sharing.

BaseballHelm implication:

- Stats Center Lite should not assume one clean official source.
- Build flexible imports for XML/CSV/manual stat lines.
- Preserve source labels and distinguish official stats from coach-entered development metrics.
- Add data quality warnings when imports conflict.

## TRAQ Owns Deep Baseball Player Development

Driveline TRAQ presents itself as a hub for player development, workouts, throwing schedules, video, goals, and technology data from hitting and pitching tools.

BaseballHelm implication:

- Do not try to out-TRAQ TRAQ in Phase 1.
- Build player timeline, development notes, practice-to-development loops, and import/attachment lanes for tech data.
- Use AI to summarize and connect data, not to pretend BaseballHelm has proprietary biomechanical analysis.

Source:

- https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/coaches/traq/
- https://help.drivelinebaseball.com/portal/en/kb/articles/what-is-traq

## Strength Platforms Are Too Deep To Clone Early

TeamBuildr, BridgeAthletic, Volt, and TrainHeroic all emphasize program design, exercise libraries, athlete mobile delivery, max tracking, readiness/questionnaires, and reporting.

BaseballHelm implication:

- Performance Lite should ingest lift assignments/results and track compliance.
- Strength staff permissions matter.
- Avoid building a full exercise library or programming engine until pilots prove demand.
- Connect lift completion, wellness, availability, and baseball workload into transparent flags.

Sources:

- https://www.teambuildr.com/
- https://www.bridgeathletic.com/
- https://voltathletics.com/college

## Final Product Direction From The Live Refresh

The most defensible one-shot strategy remains:

- baseball-specific operating layer
- import-first architecture
- player timeline
- staff meeting intelligence
- practice loop tied to stats/development/availability
- source-cited AI
- narrow, premium Phase 1 screens

The least defensible direction is:

- 18 top-level tabs
- full vendor clones
- generic chat AI
- all-in-one compliance/recruiting/strength/nutrition platform
- direct integrations before pilots
