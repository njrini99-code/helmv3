# V6 Market Research Source Log

This source log grounds the V6 stats/integrations/CoachHelm pass in current market evidence gathered on 2026-06-23.

## Official Stats and XML

- NCAA Official Baseball Statistics Rules: defines official scorer duties and official baseball statistics framing. Source: https://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/stats/Stats_Manuals/Baseball/baseball%20easy%20print%20stats%20manual.pdf
- NCAA 2025-2026 Baseball Rules Book: Rule 10 scoring references official NCAA box score and scorebook. Source: https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/sports/baseball/rules/PRMBA_RulesBook.pdf
- NCAA Division I baseball stats page: shows common official categories such as walks, batting average, complete games, doubles, ERA, and other public reporting stats. Source: https://www.ncaa.com/stats/baseball/d1
- GameChanger XML Export for College Teams: college baseball/softball staff can export game stats in standard XML accepted by PrestoSports, SIDEARM, and NCAA website workflows. Source: https://help.gc.com/hc/en-us/articles/24581262301453-XML-Export-College-Teams-Only
- StatCrew XML FAQ: documents XML output workflows and season reports/export behavior. Source: https://www.statcrew.com/faqs/faqssendxml.html

## Tracking Technology

- TrackMan Portable B1 what-we-track: highlights full ball trajectory, spin, release angles, horizontal movement, plate side, and pitching data. Source: https://www.trackman.com/baseball/Portable-B1/what-we-track
- TrackMan V3 Game Tracking what-we-track: highlights hitting launch metrics such as exit speed, spin rate, launch angle, and distance. Source: https://www.trackman.com/baseball/V3-Game-Tracking/what-we-track
- TrackMan Radar Glossary: shows pitch-level baseball fields and identities such as pitch rank, pitcher/batter, handedness, teams, and more. Source: https://support.trackmanbaseball.com/hc/en-us/articles/5089413493787-Radar-Glossary-Of-Terms
- Rapsodo baseball pages: describe hitting/pitching flight monitors, real-time data/video feedback, live-on-live, seam orientation, strike zone recognition, and spin profile concepts. Sources: https://rapsodo.com/pages/baseball and https://rapsodo.com/pages/rapsodo-baseball-pro-3-baseball-hitting-pitching-in-one
- Rapsodo exit speed and spin profile explainers: support BaseballHelm storing exit speed, spin direction/axis, and development context. Sources: https://rapsodo.com/blogs/baseball/understanding-rapsodo-hitting-data-exit-speed and https://rapsodo.com/blogs/baseball/understanding-rapsodo-pitching-data-spin-profile

## Swing Sensors

- Blast Motion baseball product and metrics pages: Blast captures swing metrics around plane, connection, rotation, impact, attack angle, bat speed, and on-plane efficiency. Sources: https://blastmotion.com/products/baseball/ and https://blastconnect.com/training-center/baseball/get-started/metrics
- Blast on-plane efficiency article: describes on-plane efficiency as percentage of swing on the swing plane using vertical bat angle at contact. Source: https://blastmotion.com/blog/what-is-on-plane-efficiency-in-baseball/
- Diamond Kinetics bat sensor/app pages: bat sensor attaches to bats, sends swing data to mobile app, and tracks hitting metrics. Sources: https://www.diamondkinetics.com/the-bat-sensor and https://www.diamondkinetics.com/the-dk-app
- MLB/Diamond Kinetics article: mentions barrel speed, acceleration, attack angle, and sensor hardware. Source: https://www.mlb.com/news/mlb-diamond-kinetics-team-up
