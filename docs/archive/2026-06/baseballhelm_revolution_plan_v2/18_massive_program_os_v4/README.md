# Massive Program OS V4

This section is the controlling upgrade for the massive build. It is based on the actual local `Downloads/helmv3` BaseballHelm structure and the market research already added in V2/V3.

V4 expands the product from a Phase 1 college-team MVP into a serious multi-program BaseballHelm platform for:

- college programs
- high school programs
- showcase organizations
- JUCO programs where relevant
- athletes connected to those organizations
- strength coaches and performance staff
- directors of operations
- academic or eligibility support roles
- recruiting/scouting workflows where they belong

The core product category remains:

BaseballHelm is the Baseball Staff Operating Graph. It connects identity, schedule, practice, game stats, player development, lifting, wellness, availability, academics, travel, documents, video links, imports, source trust, AI summaries, and staff actions into one professional team-operating system.

## Why V4 Exists

The earlier package made the plan build-ready, but it still needed a stronger "this could be a twenty million dollar product" layer:

- more exact feature contracts
- deeper strength and lifting workflows
- high school, college, showcase differences
- real settings and admin depth
- premium UI/UX requirements
- integration/import architecture
- route-by-route mapping to the current `helmv3` baseball app
- higher bar for engineering scale
