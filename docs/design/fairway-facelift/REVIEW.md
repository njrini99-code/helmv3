## Decisions

- Intelligence, Players view: stays on the DataTable (desktop) + RosterPlayerCard (phone) split. MatrixBoard has no bare row-select callback (rows only click when they expand) and its phone column-hiding set does not cover roster columns, so converting now would break tap-to-scope and reintroduce the squeezed phone table. Follow-up for the primitives pass: `onRowSelect` on MatrixBoard plus a per-board `hideOnMobile` column set; then convert.
- Spine has one verdict string and no multi-readout slot; the cockpit folds "players needing attention" and "outcomes awaiting" into one verdict sentence, and the urgent signal renders after the ledger (children slot). Accepted; a `readouts` slot and an `urgent` slot on Spine are primitives follow-ups.
