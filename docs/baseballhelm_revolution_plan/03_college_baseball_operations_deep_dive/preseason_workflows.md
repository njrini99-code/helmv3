# Preseason Workflows

## Daily reality

College baseball programs operate through fast staff communication, short prep windows, incomplete data, competing class schedules, lift schedules, field/cage constraints, travel disruptions, player development meetings, and constant roster/availability changes.

## BaseballHelm product implication

Every workflow must answer three questions:

1. What is happening today?
2. Who needs attention?
3. What changed since the last staff touchpoint?

## Data to capture

- Players involved.
- Event/session date and type.
- Coach/staff owner.
- Notes and visibility level.
- Attendance/completion.
- Player response/reflection.
- Attachments/links.
- AI summary eligibility.

## AI opportunity

CoachHelm should summarize patterns, not replace staff judgment. It should identify conflicts, missing data, trend changes, and follow-up tasks.
