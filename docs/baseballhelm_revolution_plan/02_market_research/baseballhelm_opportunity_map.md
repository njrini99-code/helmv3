# BaseballHelm Opportunity Map

## Opportunity 1: Current-team operating system

Pain: coaches manage roster, schedule, practice, lifts, availability, travel, announcements, and academics in separate places.

Build: command center, roster identity, calendar, practice planner, lifting, tasks/acknowledgements, travel, academics.

## Opportunity 2: Import-first player development layer

Pain: TrackMan/Rapsodo/Blast/HitTrax/BaseballCloud outputs do not live with coach notes, game stats, practice grades, and player goals.

Build: import center, player timeline, hitting/pitching sessions, metrics history, AI summaries.

## Opportunity 3: Lightweight strength-to-baseball bridge

Pain: strength data lives in TeamBuildr/Bridge/TrainHeroic and rarely connects to throwing workload, soreness, game usage, and readiness.

Build: lift tracker, readiness/wellness, availability, workload brief.

## Opportunity 4: AI staff meeting assistant

Pain: staff meetings require manually stitching together stats, attendance, availability, lifts, academics, and notes.

Build: CoachHelm brief, weekly team health, player meeting reports, risk flag engine.

## Opportunity 5: Player-facing daily clarity

Pain: players need to know where to be, what to complete, what to work on, and what coaches want from them.

Build: Today page, tasks, lifts, practice, goals, constructive AI summary.
