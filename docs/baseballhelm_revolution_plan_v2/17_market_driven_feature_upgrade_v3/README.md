# Market Driven Feature Upgrade V3

This section is the market-backed enhancement pass. It exists to prevent the plan from becoming "more docs" instead of a better product.

The central conclusion from the research refresh:

BaseballHelm should not win by copying Teamworks, TRAQ, TeamBuildr, GameChanger, TrackMan, Rapsodo, 6-4-3 Charts, FieldLevel, or PrestoSports. Those products already own their lanes. BaseballHelm wins by becoming the baseball-specific operating layer that turns fragmented schedules, exports, staff notes, player check-ins, practice work, game stats, lift results, academic conflicts, and AI summaries into daily baseball decisions.
