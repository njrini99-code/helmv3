# Market Research Feature Takeaways 2026

## Brutal Market Read

The market does not need another generic team app. It also does not need a half-built clone of the dominant specialty tools.

Teamworks owns broad department operations. TRAQ owns deep baseball development workflows. TrackMan and Rapsodo own trusted device-level data. 6-4-3 Charts owns deep college baseball analytics/reporting and data-source integration. TeamBuildr/Bridge/TrainHeroic own strength programming and athlete logging. Presto/GameChanger/SIDEARM own official stat and publishing workflows. FieldLevel/Perfect Game/PBR own recruiting networks and scouting databases.

BaseballHelm's wedge is not category replacement. It is category synthesis.

## What The Market Teaches BaseballHelm

### Teamworks Lesson: Daily Utility Beats Feature Count

Teamworks Hub's strongest daily pull is not fancy analytics. It is calendar, tasks, messages, travel, profiles, and athlete responsibility in one mobile place.

Feature enhancement:

- Player Today must become the daily player utility.
- Coach Command Center must become the staff decision utility.
- Calendar/tasks/acknowledgements are not boring. They are the adoption engine.
- Every feature should eventually create a task, acknowledgement, note, practice adjustment, or meeting topic.

Avoid:

- generic communications suite
- compliance engine
- department-wide configurability

### TRAQ Lesson: Development Data Needs Workflow, Not Just Charts

TRAQ is credible because it connects workouts, goals, videos, and baseball tech data into a player development workflow.

Feature enhancement:

- Player Timeline must become a development story, not an activity log.
- Player Goals should be tied to practice blocks, metrics, notes, and meeting outcomes.
- Development data should have review status: imported, reviewed, shared, actioned.
- Practice Planner should pull from development goals and recent trends.

Avoid:

- biomechanical claims
- pretending imported Rapsodo/TrackMan data is enough for advanced coaching recommendations
- huge video review surface

### TrackMan/Rapsodo Lesson: Source Trust Matters

Device platforms own measurement quality and session detail.

Feature enhancement:

- Every imported metric needs source, session type, player match confidence, and review status.
- Show a "source trust badge": official, device export, coach chart, player-entered, AI-derived.
- Player profiles should separate official game performance, practice/development metrics, and coach observations.

Avoid:

- blending official stats and development metrics in one table
- presenting AI interpretations as measured facts

### 6-4-3 Charts Lesson: Advanced Baseball Analytics Is Already A Real Category

6-4-3 integrates major baseball data sources and provides reports for college programs.

Feature enhancement:

- BaseballHelm should build "analytics-to-action" rather than deep analytics.
- Add Staff Decision Room, Postgame Action Review, and Practice Focus Builder.
- Let coaches attach/import 6-4-3/TrackMan/Synergy reports and turn findings into player tasks/practice blocks.

Avoid:

- trying to out-chart 6-4-3
- building DSR-like rankings or national data products

### TeamBuildr/Bridge/TrainHeroic Lesson: Strength Is A Full Product, Not A Tab

Strength tools own programming, athlete app logging, exercise libraries, reports, and questionnaires.

Feature enhancement:

- Performance Lite should import lift results and readiness questionnaires.
- The BaseballHelm value is cross-context: "Does this affect practice, throwing, lineup, travel, or player meeting?"
- Strength coach role should have real permissions and a focused dashboard.

Avoid:

- exercise library
- periodized programming builder
- strength marketplace

### Presto/GameChanger Lesson: Official Stats Are Operationally Messy

College stats workflows involve official exports, XML, packed files, site systems, missing stat uploads, game logs, and postgame edits.

Feature enhancement:

- Stats Center Lite needs Import Completeness, Missing Box Score, and Source Conflict states.
- Postgame workflow should have "stats imported, recap generated, player timelines updated, practice focus suggested."
- Game logs should preserve source and import run.

Avoid:

- live scoring product
- official website publishing

### Recruiting Market Lesson: Network Effects Are A Trap

FieldLevel, Perfect Game, PBR, SportsRecruits, and 6-4-3 already own parts of recruiting discovery and scouting data.

Feature enhancement:

- Recruiting should be internal roster construction, not external marketplace.
- Build a Roster Needs Board later: position depth, graduation risk, transfer needs, player development pipeline.
- Allow prospect lists and scouting metrics import later.

Avoid:

- recruiting social network
- public player profiles as Phase 1
- messaging network

## BaseballHelm's Next-Level Product Thesis

BaseballHelm is the Baseball Staff Operating Graph.

It connects:

- who the player is
- what happened
- what source says it happened
- what it means for today
- who needs to act
- what the player should see
- what gets discussed in staff/player meetings
- what changes in practice
- what appears in reports

That is the category. Not Teamworks plus stats. Not TRAQ lite. Not a dashboard pile.
