# BaseballHelm Product Summary and Sales Narrative

Generated: 2026-06-23

## The Short Version

BaseballHelm is a complete baseball program operating system for college, high school, JUCO, and showcase organizations. It is not another stats page, recruiting profile, chat tool, workout tracker, or calendar. It is the connective tissue between all of them.

Most baseball programs already have data. They have GameChanger or StatCrew stats, TrackMan or Rapsodo files, Synergy or 6-4-3 video, TeamBuildr lifts, Teamworks calendars/classes, spreadsheets, texts, PDFs, coach notes, and player videos. The problem is that none of it lives in one baseball-specific workflow. Coaches still spend hours asking:

- What changed?
- Who needs attention?
- What does that mean for practice?
- Who is unavailable?
- Which stat is official?
- Where is the video?
- Did the player actually do the thing?
- Did our adjustment work?

BaseballHelm answers those questions.

It turns sources into signals, signals into staff actions, staff actions into player workflows, and player workflows into measurable outcomes.

## Why It Is Different

The market is fragmented:

- Teamworks is strong at department-wide operations, communication, scheduling, files, workflows, logistics, and academics.
- TeamBuildr is strong at strength programming and athlete delivery.
- GameChanger/StatCrew/Presto/SIDEARM/NCAA workflows are strong at official stats and reporting.
- TrackMan/Rapsodo/Blast/Diamond Kinetics are strong at measurement.
- Synergy/6-4-3/AWRE/OnForm are strong at video and baseball analytics.
- Spreadsheets are flexible but fragile.

BaseballHelm's advantage is not pretending to replace all of those on day one. Its advantage is baseball-specific unification. It imports or links the best source data, labels its trust level, and then turns it into actual program decisions: practice blocks, player tasks, staff meeting topics, video review, availability changes, lift modifications, postPostgame Action Reviews, and development plans.

That is the product category: a baseball decision and action layer.

## Core Product Promise

BaseballHelm makes the baseball staff feel like they have a professional front office, player development department, operations coordinator, strength liaison, academic coordinator, video coordinator, and AI analyst working from the same source of truth.

For the head coach:

- Know what matters today.
- Know who needs attention.
- Know what the staff has done.
- Know where the data came from.
- Know what action to take.

For assistants:

- See only the players, signals, stations, videos, and tasks relevant to their role.
- Convert insights into practice work without recreating notes in five places.

For players:

- One simple mobile Today screen.
- Know schedule, tasks, lifts, videos, availability, and development priorities.
- No staff-only clutter.

For strength staff:

- Own lift plans and readiness without being disconnected from baseball workload, bullpens, games, and practice.

For ops/academics:

- Keep classes, travel, attendance, acknowledgements, documents, and conflicts visible before they create chaos.

## Feature System 1: Coach Command Center

### How It Works

The Coach Command Center is the staff's default landing page. It combines:

- today's schedule
- player availability
- class/lift/practice conflicts
- unread or unresolved signals
- recent imports
- postgame items
- overdue player tasks

### Why It Wins

Most dashboards show data. BaseballHelm shows decisions. A coach should not need to open Teamworks, a spreadsheet, GameChanger, TrackMan exports, Synergy clips, a lift app, and a group text to know what today requires. BaseballHelm turns that mess into one baseball staff cockpit.

## Feature System 2: Signal Inbox

### How It Works

The Signal Inbox collects source-backed alerts from stats, video, classes, lifts, readiness, tasks, imports, and CoachHelm. Each signal has:

- source
- confidence
- affected player/team
- recommended owner
- recommended action
- visibility rules
- lifecycle state
- action status

Examples:

- "Two-strike chase is up 14% over the last 18 plate appearances."
- "Friday starter's velocity and command both declined after pitch 55."
- "Three catchers have class conflicts with early defensive block."
- "Lower-body soreness plus recent workload creates a practice modification flag."
- "TrackMan import has 7 unmatched player rows."

### Why It Wins

Other products create notifications. BaseballHelm creates operationally useful signals. Every signal can become a task, practice block, lift modification, meeting item, message, video request, or dev plan item. That conversion is the magic.

## Feature System 3: Source Trust System

### How It Works

Every stat, video, import, AI output, and insight carries source metadata:

- official source

## Feature System 4: Import Dossier

### How It Works

The Import Dossier is the upload and data cleanup center. It supports CSV, XLSX, XML, PDF attachment/review, manual entry, and later API/webhook adapters.

The workflow:

1. Upload file.
2. Detect source and import type.
3. Parse raw rows.
4. Auto-map fields.
5. Match players.
6. Validate impossible values and missing fields.
7. Preview changes.
8. Commit to official/development tables.
9. Recalculate stats, signals, and timelines.
10. Roll back if needed.

Supported source presets include:

- GameChanger XML
- StatCrew/NCAA XML
- Presto/SIDEARM-style stat files
- TrackMan
- Rapsodo
- 6-4-3 Charts
- Synergy exports/reports
- AWRE references
- Blast
- Diamond Kinetics
- TeamBuildr
- Teamworks
- ArmCare
- OnForm
- Google Sheets
- generic CSV/XLSX/PDF/manual

### Why It Wins

Most platforms either own a narrow data source or force coaches into brittle spreadsheet work. BaseballHelm accepts reality: baseball programs have many tools. It becomes the place where those files become usable, trusted, audited, and actionable.

## The Competitive Pitch

BaseballHelm is better than the current market because it is not trying to be a single-purpose tool. It is the baseball-specific operating layer above the tool stack.

Against Teamworks:

- Teamworks is broad department infrastructure.
- BaseballHelm is baseball staff intelligence and action.
- BaseballHelm wins when the question is not "what is on the calendar?" but "how does today's calendar, class conflict, lift result, postgame trend, and video queue change practice?"

Against TeamBuildr:

- TeamBuildr is strength programming.
- BaseballHelm connects lifting to baseball workload, availability, readiness, practice, and player development.

Against GameChanger/StatCrew/Presto:

- Those products handle scoring/reporting.
- BaseballHelm turns official stats into postgame actions, practice changes, timelines, signals, and player tasks.

Against TrackMan/Rapsodo/Blast/Diamond Kinetics:

- Those products measure performance.
- BaseballHelm connects measurements to the program workflow.

Against Synergy/6-4-3/AWRE:

- Those products are powerful analytics/video environments.
- BaseballHelm imports or links their outputs and connects them to operations, strength, classes, player tasks, staff meetings, and CoachHelm.

Against spreadsheets:

- Spreadsheets are flexible.
- BaseballHelm is structured, permissioned, source-aware, auditable, rollback-capable, and action-oriented.

Against generic AI:

- Generic AI summarizes.
- BaseballHelm cites, assigns, tracks, and measures.
