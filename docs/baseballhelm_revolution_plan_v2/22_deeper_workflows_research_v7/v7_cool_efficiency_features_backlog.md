## "Wow" Demo Flow

1. Import GameChanger XML.
2. System creates postgame review.
3. CoachHelm detects two-strike chase and pitcher command decay.
4. Coach clicks "Generate practice plan."
5. Practice plan includes hitting station and bullpen command block.
6. Coach drags players into scrimmage lineup.
7. Plan attaches to calendar.
8. Players see their assignments in Player Today.
9. Strength coach sees two-way workload modification.
10. After practice, CoachHelm measures whether the focus improved in scrimmage/game stats.
