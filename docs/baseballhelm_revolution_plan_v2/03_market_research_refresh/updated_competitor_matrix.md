# Updated Competitor Matrix


## Core market conclusion

The market already has broad athletic department suites, official scoring tools, deep player-development devices, strength platforms, recruiting networks, and spreadsheets. BaseballHelm should not copy any single category. It should connect the outputs of those categories into baseball-specific daily decisions.

## What BaseballHelm should build

- Coach Command Center
- Player Today
- Player Timeline
- Practice Planner Lite
- Stats Center Lite with official/development separation
- Performance Lite
- Import Center MVP
- Embedded CoachHelm AI briefs/flags/summaries
- Reports for staff/player meetings

## What BaseballHelm should import

- Official game stats and season stats
- TrackMan/Rapsodo/Yakker/BaseballCloud-style pitch/hit metrics
- Lift programs/results from strength staff spreadsheets
- Wellness/readiness check-ins
- Class schedules
- Travel itineraries
- Prospect lists if recruiting is later enabled

## What BaseballHelm should attach/link

- Raw video libraries
- Full vendor dashboards
- Dense PDFs
- Medical/athletic training documents unless explicit permissions exist

## What BaseballHelm should ignore early

- Direct vendor integrations
- Full compliance workflows
- Full nutrition platform
- Recruiting marketplace
- Full exercise/drill marketplace



| Category | Tools | What they do well | Gap BaseballHelm can exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Athletic ops | Teamworks Hub, Compliance + Recruiting, Academics, Elite | Department-scale communication, compliance, academics, forms, schedules | Too broad/enterprise-heavy; not baseball-specific daily performance loop |
| Official stats | NCAA conventions, StatCrew, DakStats, PrestoStats, SIDEARM | Formal game/season/career stats | They do not connect stats to practice, lifts, wellness, player meetings |
| Team scoring | GameChanger | Easy scoring, stats, streaming, youth/team communication | Not a college baseball operating graph or staff meeting system |
| Development devices | TrackMan, Rapsodo, Yakkertech, Blast, Diamond Kinetics, BaseballCloud | Deep metrics and session history | Siloed from academics, lifts, practice plan, coach notes |
| Video | OnForm, Hudl Technique, Synergy | Analysis, sharing, clips | Video alone does not drive whole-program daily ops |
| Strength | TeamBuildr, BridgeAthletic, TrainHeroic, Smartabase, CoachMePlus | Workouts, readiness, compliance, athlete monitoring | BaseballHelm should show baseball implications, not rebuild full S&C |
| Recruiting | FieldLevel, NCSA, SportsRecruits, Perfect Game, Prep Baseball | Prospect exposure, profiles, scouting, rankings | Later internal roster-needs board; not Phase 1 product core |
| Spreadsheets | Excel, Google Sheets | Flexible, fast, familiar | No permissions, player timeline, AI brief, rollback, source-of-truth |


## BaseballHelm implication

V2 should be sold as the system that turns fragmented exports and daily staff activity into baseball decisions. It should beat competitors through specificity and integration, not breadth.
