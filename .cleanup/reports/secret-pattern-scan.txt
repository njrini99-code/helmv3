Secret-pattern scan safety note:

The Phase 14 grep was started with local env/key exclusions, but the playbook command does not exclude .cleanup/reports. The scan recursively matched its own output and expanded this file to ~39 GB, so the raw output was truncated.

Do not commit raw secret scan output. Re-run with:
- --exclude='.env*'
- --exclude='*.pem'
- --exclude='*.key'
- --exclude-dir=.cleanup
- --exclude-dir=node_modules
- --exclude-dir=.next
- --exclude-dir=.git
- --exclude-dir=dist
- --exclude-dir=build

Summaries must redact values.
