Tournaments

Design review · October 2026

Desktop and iPhone · Varsity Golf demo data

What the tab covers

Three views, all built from the same round records.

01

Events

Up next, the season ledger, and an event desk for each tournament.

02

Compare

Any two samples side by side: players, contexts, events, rounds or date ranges.

03

History

Team trend across seasons, with actual lineups or returning players only.

Events home

Every event with finish, team total and how much of it has shot data.

Who counted

Lowest four whole-round scores make the team total. NC marks a round that didn’t count.

Where the event turned

Three findings per event, each linking to the rounds behind it.

Hole analysis

Every player, every hole, by round. The three costliest holes are highlighted.

Event review

  • Each finding is split into observation, evidence and a possible reason
  • Draft a practice assignment or add to 1:1 notes from any finding
  • Coach notes and player reflections sit alongside

Part two

Comparison desk

Two independent samples, one aligned grid, every number traceable to its rounds.

Two independent scopes

A and B each set player, context and period. Presets cover the common questions.

History timeline

Every 18-hole round plotted by date against the course rating. Drag the range or click a tournament.

Stat rows

  • One card per metric, grouped by part of the game
  • Dots are rounds, the bar is the average, the dashed line is the other side
  • Difference reads in plain words: “Tournaments worse than Qualifiers”

How the numbers are computed

Every figure is derived from round records and shows its denominator.

Team totalsLowest whole-round scores each round, never best holes
Scoring average18-hole rounds only; nine-hole rounds listed as excluded
Strokes gainedShot-data rounds only, shown as “8 of 9 rounds”
Missing dataShows “—” with the reason, never a false zero
ColourGreen or amber by whether lower or higher is better
Course differencesRaw averages labelled observed; a course-rating row added

Evidence drawer

  • Opens from any value on any screen
  • Lists every round used, and any excluded with the reason
  • Click a round for its scorecard

Season history

Team scoring against course rating by event. Seasons are separate lines across the summer gap.

Incomplete data

Missing or thin data is shown as missing. No difference is drawn against it.

iPhone

Same engine, phone layout. A and B stay visible together in every block.
