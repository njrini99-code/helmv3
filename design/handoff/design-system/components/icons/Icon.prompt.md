Lucide line icon at Fairway's 1.6 stroke — use for every glyph in controls, nav and notices; lazy-loads Lucide from CDN.

```jsx
<Icon name="flag" size={16} />
<Icon name="calendar-days" size={18} strokeWidth={1.4} label="Schedule" />
```

- Names are Lucide names (https://lucide.dev/icons). Stroke 1.6 is the house weight; never fill.
- Decorative by default. Pass `label` when the icon is the only content.
