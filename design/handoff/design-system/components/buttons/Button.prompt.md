The Fairway button. One green primary per view; secondary is the workhorse; ink for a decisive neutral action.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" leftIcon="plus">Log round</Button>
<Button>Export</Button>
<Button variant="ink" size="lg">Sign scorecard</Button>
```

- Sizes: sm 30, md 36, lg 44 (touch / in-round).
- `busy` keeps the label and shows a spinner. `href` renders a link.
