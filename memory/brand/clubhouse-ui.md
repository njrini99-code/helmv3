# Clubhouse UI: type and surfaces

Decided 2026-10-21 by the owner: Clubhouse (the GolfHelm coach and player app)
keeps white cards on the ivory canvas and sets all text in Instrument Sans. This
supersedes any brand wording that asks for other card colours or another
typeface inside the product. The logo rules in `logos.md` are unchanged: the
helm mark is always green.

- **Type:** Instrument Sans only (JetBrains Mono for `kbd`). Sentence case,
  tabular numbers, a true minus, no emoji. The script "Golfhelm" wordmark is
  the one recorded exception.
- **Surfaces:** white cards (the shared Surface, `.ch-surface` in `ui.css`) on
  the ivory canvas, hairline borders, green reserved for action, selection and
  state.
- **Source of truth:** `src/clubhouse/styles/` tokens and
  `src/clubhouse/AGENTS.md`; the design handoff is `design/handoff/README.md`.
