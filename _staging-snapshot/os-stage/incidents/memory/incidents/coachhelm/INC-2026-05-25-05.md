## Summary

`WEAK_ON_GREEN_PROXIMITY_FT`, the gate for the `long_approach_3putt_cascade`
composite rule, was set to `50` — a value left over from when the field it
gates was a **percent**. The field it actually compares against,
`proximity_when_hit_feet`, is denominated in **feet**. A 50-foot bar at
175+ yards is far looser than intended, and — separately — the same
generator printed a side-by-side comparison against a PGA Tour benchmark
("Tour ~45 ft") using figures that were not the same quantity: the
research doc's Tour figure is *Proximity to Hole measured over all
approaches, misses included*; the product's `proximity_when_hit_feet` is
averaged only over shots that **found** the green, so a miss never drags
the average out. Printing them side by side told coaches their players
out-dial the Tour from 175+ yards — production mean 26 ft vs. "Tour 45
ft" — which is not a valid comparison because the two numbers exclude
different populations of shots, not because the player is unusually good.

## Root cause (verified)

Two independent defects in the same code path, both present since the
generator shipped:

1. **Unit mismatch.** `WEAK_ON_GREEN_PROXIMITY_FT = 50` was a percent-era
   constant never updated when the field it gates changed to feet.
2. **Incomparable benchmark.** The Tour anchor figures (`TOUR_PROXIMITY_FEET`:
   `{50-125ft: 18, 125-175ft: 30, 175-plus-ft: 45}`, sourced from a research
   doc's §2) measure a different population of shots (all approaches,
   misses included) than the product's own `proximity_when_hit_feet`
   (green-finding shots only). The two are not the same quantity by
   construction, and the research doc's own figures are only internally
   coherent (75.4% GIR alongside a proximity average) *because* misses are
   included in its average — a fact the product's comparison silently
   dropped.

Both defects independently made `long_approach_3putt_cascade`
**unfireable** in practice: with the old `>50` gate compared against a
percent-shaped intuition, and zero of 29 production insights in the
reviewed window exceeding 50 ft (observed max 36.3 ft), the gate never
passed.

## User impact

- Zero insights from this composite rule for its entire life to date — a
  silent availability gap, not a wrong answer, but one that would not be
  visible without directly counting `golf_coach_insights` by signature (as
  the diagnosis did).
- While the Tour comparison text existed, any coach who saw it was told a
  flattering-but-false claim: that their player's approach proximity from
  175+ yards beat the PGA Tour's. The correct like-for-like comparison
  (green-hit % vs. Tour green-hit %) was available in the same generator
  and is what remains after the fix.

## Repair

`5cd50ef87` (#1452, merged 2026-08-16T05:33:30Z):

- `WEAK_ON_GREEN_PROXIMITY_FT` corrected from `50` to `31` — the measured
  production p75 for on-green proximity from 175+ yards, derived from real
  data rather than intuition, following the same squad-percentile method
  used in `INC-2026-05-25-03`.
- The incomparable Tour-proximity benchmark (`TOUR_PROXIMITY_FEET`) was
  **removed outright** rather than corrected — the two populations cannot
  be reconciled into a valid comparison, so the code now carries an
  explicit comment (visible in the current source) documenting why no
  proximity-vs-Tour comparison exists at that call site, to stop a future
  edit from reintroducing it. The green-hit-percent vs. Tour-green-hit-percent
  comparison, which *is* like-for-like, was kept.

## Recurrence risk — the rule still does not fire after the unit fix (sibling-window territory)

**Open, not resolved by this incident's fix.** Issue #1475 (opened
2026-08-17T02:15:35Z, open as of 2026-08-21, this backfill's last check)
measured that
`long_approach_3putt_cascade` produces **zero** insights even after #1452's
correction — the gate is wired, the engine is live and writing sibling

proximity reading, is still too strict for the *subset* who also clear the
paired 3-putt condition. A second, related open issue (#1502, opened
2026-08-17T23:52:19Z) separately found the rule's citation and its
selection logic disagree about the gate distance (10-15 ft cited vs. a
different value selected). Both are dated 2026-08-17 and belong to the
08-17+ incident owner, not this entry — flagged here only so this
incident's "resolved"
status is read correctly as **unit-bug-fixed, rule-still-broken**, not as
"the composite rule works now."
