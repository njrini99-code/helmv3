## Summary

The shot tracker assigned `shot_type='approach'` by a shot's **ordinal position in
the hole** (`shot_number === par - 2`) rather than by distance, lie, or intent.
Two concrete failure shapes, both reproduced against a real round:

1. **Layups on par 5s became "approach misses."** A 270y second shot on a 520y
   par 5 is a normal layup, but the tracker labeled it `Approach · Fairway`
   and — because the recorded result was `fairway` and not `green` — forced
   the player to pick a miss direction before `Next shot` would enable. There
   was no "laid up, not a miss" option in the 3×3 grid (Long/Short/Left/Right
   only).
2. **A penalty on the tee re-labeled the replayed tee shot as an approach.**
   After `+ Penalty` on a par-4, 375y hole, the tracker wrote shot 1 as
   `shot_type='penalty'` and shot 2 — still played from the tee, still 375y
   out — as `shot_type='approach'`, `lie_before='tee'`, `miss_direction='short'`.

In the one round used to find this, **4 of 8 `approach_miss_details` rows were
not approach shots at all.**

## Root cause (verified)

`golf-stats-calculator-shots.ts` classified a shot as an approach purely from
its ordinal (`shot_number === par - 2`), with no check against the shot's
actual distance, lie, or whether it made material progress toward the hole.
Any shot occupying "the expected approach slot" by count was written as an
approach, regardless of what it actually was.

## User impact

- Approach-miss data (`approach_miss_details`) was corrupted for any round
  containing a par-5 layup or a post-penalty replayed tee shot — both common,
  ordinary golf situations, not edge cases.
- Downstream: approach stats, miss-bias analysis, and anything reading
  `approach_miss_details` (stats pages, CoachHelm shot-driver/pattern
  analysis) inherited the corruption silently — no error, no flag, just
  wrong labels feeding real statistics.
- UX cost during play: a player who deliberately laid up was forced to pick
  a miss direction that did not happen before the tracker would let them
  continue.

## Repair

**Commit `a93b0d988`, PR #1221, landed 2026-08-02T16:02:40Z** (`git log`
author date `2026-08-02T12:02:40-04:00`).

Extracted the classification rule into a single shared function,
`src/lib/golf/approach-plausibility.ts` — distance ≤ 250y, a real approach
lie (with `tee` allowed only on par 3s, where the tee shot *is* the
approach), and material progress toward the hole — and used it in all three
places that had been able to drift apart:

- **`golf-stats-calculator-shots.ts`** — the consumer with no guard at all,
  and the one that produced the 375y "approach" from a fairway lie. The
  ordinal is now a *hint* only: the candidate is validated, and on failure
  the code falls through to the shot that actually reached the green.
- **`golf.ts` write path**, both call sites (`submitGolfRoundComprehensive`
  and `savePartialRoundImpl`, which carried the identical bug) — bad rows no
  longer reach `approach_miss_details` at write time.
- **`approach-analytics.ts`** — its local `isRealApproach` now delegates to
  the shared rule instead of re-deriving its own copy.

New test `src/lib/golf/__tests__/approach-plausibility.test.ts` (19 cases)
pins the exact shapes from the real round — the three par-5 layups, the 375y
post-penalty tee shot, no-progress rows — and asserts genuine approaches
(including par-3 tee shots and greenside chips) still count.

## Recurrence risk

Two gaps were explicitly left open by the fix author rather than closed
silently — both still true as of this incident's authoring, on available
records:

- **No "laid up" option in the tracker UI.** The fix stops bad data being
  written and aggregated, but the tracker still demands a miss direction
  from a player who laid up on purpose; `Next shot` still won't enable
  without picking Long/Short/Left/Right. The assertion is now discarded by
  the stats layer rather than believed, but the in-flow UX friction remains.
  Author's own note: "worth its own issue if you want it" — no evidence a
  follow-up issue was filed.
- **Historical bad rows not cleaned up.** `approach_miss_details` still
  contained the pre-fix corrupted rows (4 of 8 in the inspected round) at
  fix time. Read paths were said to filter them going forward, so
  player-facing stats read correctly, but no migration was run to correct
  or remove the stored rows themselves — noted as "optional, not urgent" by
  the author, not verified as done since.

## Invariant candidate

Yes — a bounded, read-only check for the exact corrupted shape:

```sql
-- INV-SHOT-001: an approach_miss_details row whose paired shot's
-- lie_before is 'tee' should only exist on a par-3 hole (where the tee
-- shot IS the approach). A tee-lie approach row on a par-4/par-5 is the
-- exact "post-penalty replayed tee shot mislabeled as approach" shape.
select amd.id, gs.round_id, gh.par, gs.lie_before
from approach_miss_details amd
join golf_shots gs on gs.id = amd.shot_id
join golf_holes gh on gh.id = gs.hole_id
where gs.lie_before = 'tee'
  and gh.par != 3
  and gs.created_at > now() - interval '24 hours';
```

Severity: MEDIUM (data-integrity class, but the write-path fix already
closes the intake; this is a guard against a future regression of the same
shape, not evidence of ongoing corruption).
