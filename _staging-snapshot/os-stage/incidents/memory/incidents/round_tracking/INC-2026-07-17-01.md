## Summary

A single QA sweep on 2026-07-17 found four distinct data-hygiene defects
across the round-tracking surface:

1. **Date off-by-one.** Round dates displayed inconsistently between
   surfaces depending on timezone handling at render time — two components
   (`FairwayRoundDetail`, `FairwayRoundRow`) could disagree on which
   calendar day a round happened on.
2. **Back nine mislabeled "OUT".** Both nines of a round could render with
   the same "OUT" label rather than distinguishing front (OUT) from back (IN).
3. **Free-text course names produced near-duplicates.** Because course
   names were stored as free text rather than referencing a canonical course
   entity, the same physical course could appear under multiple spellings —
   at the time, 5 course-name variants needing canonicalization.
4. **Orphaned qualifier-tagged rounds.** Rounds that should have been tagged
   `round_type='qualifier'` were not consistently tagged, leaving qualifier
   rounds effectively orphaned from the qualifier they belonged to (2 known
   affected rounds on the demo team at filing time).

## Root cause (verified for the fixed portion)

- **Date off-by-one:** no shared, UTC-pinned date-only parsing/formatting
  existed; each surface derived the display date independently, subject to
  local-timezone drift at render time.
- **Putts-per-round / nine-labeling:** no shared, tested utility existed for
  splitting a round's holes into front/back nine and computing derived
  per-nine stats consistently.

## User impact

- **Confirmed fixed:** players and coaches previously could see a round's
  date differ by one day depending which page they viewed it from, and could
  see the back nine mislabeled as "OUT" instead of "IN" — both directly
  visible, both corrected same-day.
- **Confirmed NOT verified fixed at closing time:** whether the qualifier-
  round backfill script had actually been executed against production
  (rather than merely merged as code) was an open question explicitly
  flagged in the 2026-07-18 audit comment and never answered in available
  records before the issue was closed as stale 11 days later. If the
  backfill was never run, the 2 originally-reported orphaned qualifier
  rounds — and any created since — may still be untagged. This directly
  touches the `qualifiers` feature: a round that should link to a qualifier
  but doesn't is invisible to qualifier-scoped views and standings.
  **This miner did not independently verify, as of 2026-08-21 (this
  record's authoring date), whether the backfill script has since been
  run.**
- Course-name canonicalization (the 5 known variants) — same evidentiary
  gap: fixed in the sense that new code exists (`scripts/refresh-golf-demo-realism.ts`
  and related), unconfirmed in the sense of whether it was actually executed
  against the affected rows.

  does not prevent a 6th free-text variant appearing on the next round entry.

## Invariant candidate

Yes, targeting the specific unresolved-verification gap:

```sql
-- INV-ROUND-004: a completed round whose qualifier belongs to should carry
-- round_type='qualifier'. Cross-check against whatever mechanism links a
-- round to a qualifier (qualifier_id or equivalent) to catch orphaned

select r.id, r.player_id, r.round_type, r.qualifier_id
from golf_rounds r
where r.qualifier_id is not null
  and r.round_type is distinct from 'qualifier'
  and r.created_at > now() - interval '30 days';
```

Severity: MEDIUM — this is exactly the class of drift #916 reported and the
backfill script was meant to close; running this now would establish
whether the gap is still open.
